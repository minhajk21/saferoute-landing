Is Mission Lake safe?

Mission Lake sits at the safer end of Kansas City by reported crime.     Common questions

    Is Mission Lake safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Mission Lake are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Mission Lake?

    Violent crime — 14 of 54 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Mission Lake safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
