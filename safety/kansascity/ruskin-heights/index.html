Is Ruskin Heights safe?

Ruskin Heights records more reported crime than most Kansas City neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 32 out of 100 — 23 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 174th of 244 Kansas City neighborhoods — based on 383 incidents reported to the KCPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Ruskin Heights safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Ruskin Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Ruskin Heights?

    Violent crime — 161 of 383 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Ruskin Heights safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
