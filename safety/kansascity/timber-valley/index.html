Timber Valley sits at the safer end of Kansas City by reported crime.     Common questions

    Is Timber Valley safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 87/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Timber Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Timber Valley?

    Violent crime — 12 of 33 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Timber Valley safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
