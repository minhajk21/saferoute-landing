Is Westside South safe?

Westside South records more reported crime than most Kansas City neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 11 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 154th of 244 Kansas City neighborhoods — based on 260 incidents reported to the KCPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Westside South safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 68% of severity-weighted incidents in Westside South are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Westside South?

    Violent crime — 56 of 260 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Westside South safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
