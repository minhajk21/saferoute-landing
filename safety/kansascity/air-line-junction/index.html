Common questions

Is Air Line Junction safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Air Line Junction are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Air Line Junction?

Vehicle crime — 33 of 96 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Air Line Junction safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
