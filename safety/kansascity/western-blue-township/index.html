Is Western Blue Township safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Western Blue Township are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Western Blue Township?

Violent crime — 13 of 43 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Western Blue Township safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
