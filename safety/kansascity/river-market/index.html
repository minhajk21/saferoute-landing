River Market records a high level of reported crime for Kansas City.     Common questions

    Is River Market safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 22/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in River Market are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in River Market?

    Violent crime — 113 of 817 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the River Market safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
