Is Minneville safe?

Minneville sits at the safer end of Kansas City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 96 out of 100 — 41 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 6th of 243 Kansas City neighborhoods — based on 10 incidents reported to the KCPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Minneville safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 96/100). About 27% of severity-weighted incidents in Minneville are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Minneville?

    Vehicle crime — 5 of 10 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Minneville safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
