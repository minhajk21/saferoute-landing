Is Little Blue safe?

Little Blue sits at the safer end of Kansas City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 97 out of 100 — 42 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 3rd of 243 Kansas City neighborhoods — based on 8 incidents reported to the KCPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026. Much of this area is industrial, river-bottom or undeveloped land rather than housing, so a low count reflects how few people are here rather than how safe the streets are.

    Common questions

    Is Little Blue safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 97/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Little Blue are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Little Blue?

    Vehicle crime — 3 of 8 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Little Blue safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
