Hickman Mills records more reported crime than most Kansas City neighborhoods.     Is Hickman Mills safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Hickman Mills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Hickman Mills?

    Violent crime — 68 of 252 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Hickman Mills safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
