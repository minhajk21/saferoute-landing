    Common questions

    Is Manheim Park safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Manheim Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Manheim Park?

    Violent crime — 130 of 602 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Manheim Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
