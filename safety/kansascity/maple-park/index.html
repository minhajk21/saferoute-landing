Is Maple Park safe?

Maple Park sits at the safer end of Kansas City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 23 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 45th of 243 Kansas City neighborhoods — based on 56 incidents reported to the KCPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Maple Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Maple Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Kansas City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Maple Park?

Violent crime — 19 of 56 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Maple Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
