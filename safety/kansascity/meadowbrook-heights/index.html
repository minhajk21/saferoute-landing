    Is Meadowbrook Heights safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 67% of severity-weighted incidents in Meadowbrook Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Meadowbrook Heights?

    Violent crime — 25 of 89 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Meadowbrook Heights safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Kansas City Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Kansas City, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
