Common questions

Is Hillside Oaks safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Hillside Oaks are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hillside Oaks?

Vehicle crime — 96 of 379 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Hillside Oaks safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
