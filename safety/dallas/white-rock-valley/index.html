Is White Rock Valley safe?

White Rock Valley sits at the safer end of Dallas by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 83 out of 100 — 28 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 12th of 188 Dallas neighborhoods — based on 123 incidents reported to the Dallas Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is White Rock Valley safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 83/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in White Rock Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Dallas, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in White Rock Valley?

Vehicle crime — 39 of 123 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the White Rock Valley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
