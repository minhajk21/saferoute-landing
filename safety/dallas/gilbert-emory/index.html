Gilbert-Emory records more reported crime than most Dallas neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Gilbert-Emory safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Gilbert-Emory are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Gilbert-Emory?

Vehicle crime — 333 of 1,153 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Gilbert-Emory safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
