Is Hillview Terrace safe?

Hillview Terrace records more reported crime than most Dallas neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Hillview Terrace safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Hillview Terrace are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hillview Terrace?

Vehicle crime — 178 of 825 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Hillview Terrace safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
