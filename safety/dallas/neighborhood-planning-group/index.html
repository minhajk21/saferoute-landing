Common questions

Is Neighborhood Planning Group safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Neighborhood Planning Group are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Dallas, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Neighborhood Planning Group?

Other — 67 of 147 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Neighborhood Planning Group safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
