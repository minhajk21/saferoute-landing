Is Lakewood, Dallas safe?

Lakewood sits at the safer end of Dallas by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 18th of 188 Dallas neighborhoods — based on 129 incidents reported to the Dallas Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Lakewood safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in Lakewood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Dallas, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Lakewood?

Vehicle crime — 67 of 129 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Lakewood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
