Is Polk Terrace safe?

Polk Terrace records more reported crime than most Dallas neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Polk Terrace safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 64% of severity-weighted incidents in Polk Terrace are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Polk Terrace?

Vehicle crime — 138 of 514 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Polk Terrace safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
