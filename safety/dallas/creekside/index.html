Is Creekside safe?

Creekside records more reported crime than most Dallas neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 148th of 188 Dallas neighborhoods — based on 696 incidents reported to the Dallas Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Creekside safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Creekside are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Creekside?

Vehicle crime — 201 of 696 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Creekside safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
