Is L Streets safe?

L Streets shows a typical level of reported crime for Dallas. Common questions

Is L Streets safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in L Streets are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in L Streets?

Other — 105 of 257 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the L Streets safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
