Common questions

Is Kleberg safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Kleberg are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Kleberg?

Other — 84 of 224 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Kleberg safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
