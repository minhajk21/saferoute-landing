Is Royal Northaven safe?

Royal Northaven sits at the safer end of Dallas by reported crime. Common questions

Is Royal Northaven safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Royal Northaven are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Dallas, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Royal Northaven?

Vehicle crime — 26 of 59 incidents (44%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to August 2026.

How is the Royal Northaven safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
