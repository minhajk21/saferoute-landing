Is Greenland Hills safe?

Greenland Hills records more reported crime than most Dallas neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 9 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 134th of 188 Dallas neighborhoods — based on 615 incidents reported to the Dallas Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Greenland Hills safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Greenland Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greenland Hills?

Vehicle crime — 285 of 615 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Greenland Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
