Common questions

Is Lakewood Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 60/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Lakewood Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lakewood Heights?

Vehicle crime — 186 of 352 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Lakewood Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
