Is Moss Farm safe?

Moss Farm sits at the safer end of Dallas by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 35th of 188 Dallas neighborhoods — based on 204 incidents reported to the Dallas Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Moss Farm safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in Moss Farm are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Moss Farm?

Vehicle crime — 56 of 204 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 11 months to September 2026.

How is the Moss Farm safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Dallas Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last twelve months within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Dallas, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
