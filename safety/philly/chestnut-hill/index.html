Is Chestnut Hill safe?

Chestnut Hill sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 93 out of 100 — 39 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 7th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 86 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Chestnut Hill safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 93/100). About 29% of severity-weighted incidents in Chestnut Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Chestnut Hill?

Violent crime — 23 of 86 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Chestnut Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
