Common questions

Is Oxford Circle safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Oxford Circle are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Oxford Circle?

Violent crime — 239 of 917 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Oxford Circle safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
