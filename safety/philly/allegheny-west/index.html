Common questions

Is Allegheny West safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Allegheny West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Allegheny West?

Violent crime — 274 of 953 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Allegheny West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
