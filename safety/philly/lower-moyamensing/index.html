Is Lower Moyamensing safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Lower Moyamensing are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lower Moyamensing?

Violent crime — 347 of 1,427 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Lower Moyamensing safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
