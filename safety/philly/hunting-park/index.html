Common questions

Is Hunting Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 28% of severity-weighted incidents in Hunting Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hunting Park?

Violent crime — 387 of 1,822 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Hunting Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
