Is East Passyunk safe?

East Passyunk records more reported crime than most Philadelphia neighborhoods. Common questions

Is East Passyunk safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in East Passyunk are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Passyunk?

Violent crime — 339 of 1,496 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the East Passyunk safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
