Common questions

Is Graduate Hospital safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in Graduate Hospital are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Graduate Hospital?

Other theft — 991 of 1,892 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Graduate Hospital safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
