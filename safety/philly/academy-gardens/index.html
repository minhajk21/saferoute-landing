Is Academy Gardens safe?

Academy Gardens sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 23 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 36th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 490 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Academy Gardens safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Academy Gardens are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Academy Gardens?

Other theft — 306 of 490 incidents (62%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Academy Gardens safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
