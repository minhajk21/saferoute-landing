Is Wissahickon Hills safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in Wissahickon Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Wissahickon Hills?

Violent crime — 48 of 255 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Wissahickon Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
