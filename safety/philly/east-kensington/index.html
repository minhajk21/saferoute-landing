Common questions

Is East Kensington safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in East Kensington are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Kensington?

Violent crime — 372 of 2,027 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the East Kensington safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
