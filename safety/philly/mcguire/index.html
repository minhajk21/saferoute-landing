Common questions

Is McGuire safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in McGuire are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in McGuire?

Violent crime — 545 of 2,781 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the McGuire safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
