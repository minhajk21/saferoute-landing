Common questions

Is Dickinson Narrows safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 50/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Dickinson Narrows are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Dickinson Narrows?

Violent crime — 298 of 1,597 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Dickinson Narrows safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
