Common questions

Is Pennsport safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in Pennsport are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pennsport?

Other theft — 602 of 1,334 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Pennsport safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
