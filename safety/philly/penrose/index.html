Common questions

Is Penrose safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Penrose are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Penrose?

Violent crime — 172 of 671 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Penrose safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
