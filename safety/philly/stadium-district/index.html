Common questions

Is Stadium District safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Stadium District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Stadium District?

Violent crime — 67 of 227 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Stadium District safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
