Common questions

Is Crescentville safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 60/100). About 34% of severity-weighted incidents in Crescentville are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Crescentville?

Violent crime — 213 of 1,046 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Crescentville safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
