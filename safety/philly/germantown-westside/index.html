Common questions

Is Germantown - Westside safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Germantown - Westside are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Germantown - Westside?

Violent crime — 272 of 1,101 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Germantown - Westside safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
