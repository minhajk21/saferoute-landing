Is Ludlow safe?

Ludlow records more reported crime than most Philadelphia neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 118th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 1,743 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Ludlow safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Ludlow are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ludlow?

Violent crime — 368 of 1,743 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Ludlow safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
