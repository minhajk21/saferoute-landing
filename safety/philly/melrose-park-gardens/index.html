Common questions

Is Melrose Park Gardens safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 33% of severity-weighted incidents in Melrose Park Gardens are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Melrose Park Gardens?

Violent crime — 118 of 528 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Melrose Park Gardens safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
