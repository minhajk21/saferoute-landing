Common questions

Is Spring Garden safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 50/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in Spring Garden are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Spring Garden?

Violent crime — 282 of 1,369 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Spring Garden safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
