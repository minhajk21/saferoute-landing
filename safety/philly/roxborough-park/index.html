Is Roxborough Park safe?

Roxborough Park sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Common questions

Is Roxborough Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 87/100). About 34% of severity-weighted incidents in Roxborough Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Roxborough Park?

Other theft — 123 of 236 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Roxborough Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
