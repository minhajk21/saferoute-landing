Common questions

Is Juniata Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Juniata Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Juniata Park?

Violent crime — 213 of 1,026 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Juniata Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
