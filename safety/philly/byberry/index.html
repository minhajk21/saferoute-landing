Is Byberry safe?

Byberry sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 98 out of 100 — 44 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 2nd of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 29 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026. Most of this area is industrial, institutional and commercial land rather than housing, so a low count reflects how few people live here rather than how safe the streets are.

Common questions

Is Byberry safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 98/100). About 15% of severity-weighted incidents in Byberry are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Byberry?

Violent crime — 9 of 29 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Byberry safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
