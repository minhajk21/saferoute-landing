Common questions

Is East Parkside safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in East Parkside are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Parkside?

Violent crime — 369 of 1,003 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the East Parkside safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
