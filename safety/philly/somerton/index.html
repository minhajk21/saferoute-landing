Is Somerton safe?

Somerton sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 89 out of 100 — 34 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 11th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 166 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Somerton safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Somerton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Somerton?

Violent crime — 40 of 166 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Somerton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
