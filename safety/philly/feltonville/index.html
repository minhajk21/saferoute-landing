Is Feltonville safe?

Feltonville records more reported crime than most Philadelphia neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Feltonville safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 28% of severity-weighted incidents in Feltonville are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Feltonville?

Violent crime — 270 of 1,399 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Feltonville safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
