Common questions

Is Cedarbrook safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Cedarbrook are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Cedarbrook?

Violent crime — 151 of 488 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Cedarbrook safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
