Is Franklin Mills safe?

Franklin Mills sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 84 out of 100 — 30 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 23rd of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 280 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Franklin Mills safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 84/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Franklin Mills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Franklin Mills?

Other theft — 143 of 280 incidents (51%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Franklin Mills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
