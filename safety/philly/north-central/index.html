Is North Central safe?

North Central records a high level of reported crime for Philadelphia. Common questions

Is North Central safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 29/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in North Central are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Central?

Violent crime — 826 of 3,354 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the North Central safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
