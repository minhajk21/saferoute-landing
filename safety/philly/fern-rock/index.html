Common questions

Is Fern Rock safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 53/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Fern Rock are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Fern Rock?

Violent crime — 367 of 1,260 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Fern Rock safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
