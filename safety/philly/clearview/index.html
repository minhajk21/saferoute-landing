Common questions

Is Clearview safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Clearview are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Clearview?

Violent crime — 93 of 406 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Clearview safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
