Is West Torresdale safe?

West Torresdale sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 87 out of 100 — 33 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 15th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 209 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is West Torresdale safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 87/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in West Torresdale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in West Torresdale?

Violent crime — 47 of 209 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the West Torresdale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
