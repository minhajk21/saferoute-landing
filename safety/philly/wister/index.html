Common questions

Is Wister safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Wister are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Wister?

Violent crime — 323 of 1,229 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Wister safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
