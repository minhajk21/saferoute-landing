Is East Poplar safe?

East Poplar records more reported crime than most Philadelphia neighborhoods. Common questions

Is East Poplar safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in East Poplar are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Poplar?

Violent crime — 316 of 1,436 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the East Poplar safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
