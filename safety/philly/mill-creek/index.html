Common questions

Is Mill Creek safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Mill Creek are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mill Creek?

Violent crime — 576 of 1,655 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Mill Creek safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
