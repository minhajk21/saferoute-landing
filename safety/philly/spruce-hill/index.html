Common questions

Is Spruce Hill safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Spruce Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Spruce Hill?

Violent crime — 257 of 1,145 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Spruce Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
