Is Center City East safe?

Common questions

Is Center City East safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in Center City East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Center City East?

Violent crime — 840 of 4,052 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Center City East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
