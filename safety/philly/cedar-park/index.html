Common questions

Is Cedar Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 53/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Cedar Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Cedar Park?

Violent crime — 318 of 1,116 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Cedar Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
