Is Dearnley Park safe?

Dearnley Park sits at the safer end of Philadelphia by reported crime. Common questions

Is Dearnley Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 95/100). About 24% of severity-weighted incidents in Dearnley Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Philadelphia, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Dearnley Park?

Other theft — 34 of 83 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Dearnley Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
