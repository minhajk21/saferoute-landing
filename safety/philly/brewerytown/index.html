Is Brewerytown safe?

Brewerytown records more reported crime than most Philadelphia neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Brewerytown safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Brewerytown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brewerytown?

Violent crime — 510 of 1,642 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Brewerytown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Philadelphia, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
