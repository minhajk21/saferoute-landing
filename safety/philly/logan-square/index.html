Is Logan Square safe?

Logan Square records a high level of reported crime for Philadelphia. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 13 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 129th of 151 Philadelphia neighborhoods — based on 2,055 incidents reported to the Philadelphia Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Logan Square safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in Logan Square are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Logan Square?

Violent crime — 426 of 2,055 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Logan Square safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Philadelphia Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
