Is New Aurora - English Turn safe?

New Aurora - English Turn sits at the safer end of New Orleans by dispatched 911 calls. Its SafeRoute safety index is 98 out of 100 — 42 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 1st of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 5 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to May 2026. Most of this area is parkland and undeveloped ground rather than homes, so a low count reflects how few people live here rather than how safe the streets are.

    What is the most common crime in New Aurora - English Turn?

    How is the New Aurora - English Turn safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
