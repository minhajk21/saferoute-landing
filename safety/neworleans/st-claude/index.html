Is St. Claude safe?

St. Claude records more dispatched 911 calls than most New Orleans neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 35 out of 100 — 21 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 59th of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 828 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is St. Claude safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in St. Claude are dispatched between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in St. Claude?

    Violent crime — 232 of 828 incidents (28%) dispatched within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the St. Claude safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
