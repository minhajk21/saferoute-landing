Is Audubon safe?

Audubon sits at the safer end of New Orleans by dispatched 911 calls. Its SafeRoute safety index is 82 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 12th of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 106 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Audubon safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 82/100). About 26% of severity-weighted incidents in Audubon are dispatched between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Few crime-related 911 calls are dispatched here, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Audubon?

    Violent crime — 18 of 106 incidents (17%) dispatched within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Audubon safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
