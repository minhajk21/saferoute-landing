Is Central City safe?

Central City records a high level of dispatched 911 calls for New Orleans. Its SafeRoute safety index is 19 out of 100 — 37 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 67th of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 1,932 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Central City safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 19/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Central City are dispatched between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Central City?

    Violent crime — 609 of 1,932 incidents (32%) dispatched within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Central City safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
