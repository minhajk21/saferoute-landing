Is West Riverside safe?

West Riverside sits at the safer end of New Orleans by dispatched 911 calls. Its SafeRoute safety index is 85 out of 100 — 29 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 11th of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 134 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is West Riverside safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in West Riverside are dispatched between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Few crime-related 911 calls are dispatched here, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in West Riverside?

    Violent crime — 14 of 134 incidents (10%) dispatched within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the West Riverside safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
