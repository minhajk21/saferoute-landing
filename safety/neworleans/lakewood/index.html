Is Lakewood safe?

Lakewood sits at the safer end of New Orleans by dispatched 911 calls. Its SafeRoute safety index is 90 out of 100 — 34 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 5th of 70 New Orleans neighborhoods — based on 62 incidents dispatched to the NOPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Lakewood safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 90/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Lakewood are dispatched between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Few crime-related 911 calls are dispatched here, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Lakewood?

    Violent crime — 14 of 62 incidents (23%) dispatched within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Lakewood safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each crime-related 911 call the NOPD was dispatched to by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. New Orleans is scored on dispatch calls rather than filed reports, because NOPD's report series carries no location data — so a call here means police were sent, not that an offense was confirmed. It is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New Orleans, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
