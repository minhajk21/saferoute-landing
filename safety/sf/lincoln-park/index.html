Is Lincoln Park safe?

Lincoln Park sits at the safer end of San Francisco by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 85 out of 100 — 35 points above the citywide median of 50, ranking 4th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 129 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Lincoln Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Lincoln Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Lincoln Park?

Other theft — 47 of 129 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lincoln Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
