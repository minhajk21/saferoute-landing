Is Mission Bay safe?

Mission Bay records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 40 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 50, ranking 29th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 874 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Mission Bay safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Mission Bay are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mission Bay?

Violent crime — 114 of 874 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Mission Bay safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
