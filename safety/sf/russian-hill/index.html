Is Russian Hill safe?

Russian Hill records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 43 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 30th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 907 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Russian Hill safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Russian Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Russian Hill?

Other theft — 354 of 907 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Russian Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
