Is Marina safe?

Marina records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 51 out of 100 — 6 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 26th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 702 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Marina safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Marina are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Marina?

Other theft — 328 of 702 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Marina safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
