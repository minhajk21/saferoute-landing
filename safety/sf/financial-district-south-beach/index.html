Common questions

Is Financial District/South Beach safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 29/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Financial District/South Beach are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Financial District/South Beach?

Violent crime — 241 of 1,729 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Financial District/South Beach safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
