Is Chinatown safe?

Chinatown records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 27 out of 100 — 23 points below the citywide median of 50, ranking 34th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 1,589 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Chinatown safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Chinatown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Chinatown?

Violent crime — 202 of 1,589 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Chinatown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
