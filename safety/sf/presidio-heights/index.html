Is Presidio Heights safe?

Presidio Heights shows a typical level of reported crime for San Francisco. Common questions

Is Presidio Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Presidio Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Presidio Heights?

Other theft — 183 of 438 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Presidio Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
