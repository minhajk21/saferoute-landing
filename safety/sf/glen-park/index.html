Is Glen Park safe?

Common questions

Is Glen Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Glen Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Glen Park?

Other theft — 85 of 203 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Glen Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
