Is Sunset/Parkside safe?

Sunset/Parkside sits at the safer end of San Francisco by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 82 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 6th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 155 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Sunset/Parkside safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 82/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Sunset/Parkside are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Sunset/Parkside?

Other theft — 70 of 155 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Sunset/Parkside safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
