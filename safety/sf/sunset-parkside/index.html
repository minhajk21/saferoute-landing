Is Sunset/Parkside safe?

Sunset/Parkside sits at the safer end of San Francisco by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 79 out of 100 — 29 points above the citywide median of 50, ranking 6th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 185 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Sunset/Parkside safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 79/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Sunset/Parkside are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Sunset/Parkside?

Other theft — 84 of 185 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Sunset/Parkside safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
