Is Noe Valley safe?

Noe Valley shows a typical level of reported crime for San Francisco. Common questions

Is Noe Valley safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 58/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Noe Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Noe Valley?

Other theft — 202 of 499 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Noe Valley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
