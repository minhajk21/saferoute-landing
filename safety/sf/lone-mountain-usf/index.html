Common questions

Is Lone Mountain/USF safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Lone Mountain/USF are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lone Mountain/USF?

Other theft — 251 of 620 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lone Mountain/USF safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
