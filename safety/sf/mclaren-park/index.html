Common questions

Is McLaren Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in McLaren Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in McLaren Park?

Violent crime — 41 of 203 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the McLaren Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
