Is Western Addition safe?

Western Addition records a high level of reported crime for San Francisco. Its SafeRoute safety index is 31 out of 100 — 26 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 35th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 1,602 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Western Addition safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Western Addition are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Western Addition?

Other theft — 659 of 1,602 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Western Addition safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
