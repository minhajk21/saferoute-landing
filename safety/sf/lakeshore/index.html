Is Lakeshore safe?

Lakeshore sits at the safer end of San Francisco by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 89 out of 100 — 39 points above the citywide median of 50, ranking 2nd of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 79 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Lakeshore safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Lakeshore are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Lakeshore?

Other theft — 42 of 79 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lakeshore safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
