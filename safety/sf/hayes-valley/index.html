Is Hayes Valley safe?

Hayes Valley records a high level of reported crime for San Francisco. Common questions

Is Hayes Valley safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 23/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Hayes Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hayes Valley?

Other theft — 672 of 1,996 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Hayes Valley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
