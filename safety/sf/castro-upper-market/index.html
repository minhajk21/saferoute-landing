Is Castro/Upper Market safe?

Castro/Upper Market records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 31st of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 989 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Castro/Upper Market safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Castro/Upper Market are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Castro/Upper Market?

Other theft — 396 of 989 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Castro/Upper Market safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
