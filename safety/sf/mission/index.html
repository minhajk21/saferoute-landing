Is Mission safe?

Mission records a high level of reported crime for San Francisco. Its SafeRoute safety index is 17 out of 100 — 40 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 38th of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 3,320 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Mission safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 17/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Mission are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mission?

Drugs — 936 of 3,320 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Mission safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
