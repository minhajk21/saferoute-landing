Is Treasure Island safe?

Treasure Island sits at the safer end of San Francisco by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 89 out of 100 — 39 points above the citywide median of 50, ranking 3rd of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 66 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Treasure Island safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Treasure Island are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Francisco, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Treasure Island?

Violent crime — 10 of 66 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Treasure Island safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
