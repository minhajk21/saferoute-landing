Common questions

Is Bernal Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Bernal Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bernal Heights?

Violent crime — 65 of 554 incidents (12%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Bernal Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
