Common questions

Is Visitacion Valley safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Visitacion Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Visitacion Valley?

Violent crime — 64 of 340 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Visitacion Valley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
