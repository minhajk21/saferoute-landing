Common questions

Is Haight Ashbury safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Haight Ashbury are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Haight Ashbury?

Other theft — 291 of 658 incidents (44%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Haight Ashbury safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
