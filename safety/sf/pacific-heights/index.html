Is Pacific Heights safe?

Pacific Heights records more reported crime than most San Francisco neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 35 out of 100 — 15 points below the citywide median of 50, ranking 32nd of 41 SF neighborhoods — based on 1,412 incidents reported to the SFPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Pacific Heights safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Pacific Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pacific Heights?

Other theft — 641 of 1,412 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Pacific Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
