Common questions

Is Inner Sunset safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Inner Sunset are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Inner Sunset?

Other theft — 101 of 221 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Inner Sunset safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Francisco, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
