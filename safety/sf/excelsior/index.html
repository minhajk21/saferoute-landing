Common questions

Is Excelsior safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Excelsior are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Excelsior?

Violent crime — 74 of 459 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Excelsior safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the SFPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
