Is Kirkstall safe at night?

What is the most common crime in Kirkstall?

Violent crime — 27 of 82 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

How is the Kirkstall safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Leeds rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Leeds, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
