Is Harewood safe?

Harewood sits at the safer end of Leeds by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 94 out of 100 — 40 points above the Leeds median of 54, ranking 1st of 33 Leeds wards — based on 8 incidents reported to West Yorkshire Police within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026. Most of this ward is farmland and village rather than town, so a low count reflects how few people are here rather than how safe the streets are.

How is the Harewood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Leeds rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Leeds, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
