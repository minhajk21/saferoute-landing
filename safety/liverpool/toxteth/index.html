Is Toxteth safe?

Toxteth records more reported crime than most Liverpool wards. Its SafeRoute safety index is 34 out of 100 — 21 points below the Liverpool median of 55, ranking 53rd of 64 Liverpool wards — based on 333 incidents reported to Merseyside Police within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

    Is Toxteth safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 34/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Toxteth?

    Violent crime — 136 of 333 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

    How is the Toxteth safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Liverpool rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Liverpool, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
