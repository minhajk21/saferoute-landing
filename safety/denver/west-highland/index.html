Is West Highland safe?

West Highland records more reported crime than most Denver neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 52 out of 100 — right at the citywide median of 55, ranking 47th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 336 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is West Highland safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in West Highland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in West Highland?

    Vehicle crime — 118 of 336 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the West Highland safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
