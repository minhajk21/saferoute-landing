Is Hampden safe?

Hampden sits at the safer end of Denver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 15th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 134 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Hampden safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 29% of severity-weighted incidents in Hampden are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Hampden?

    Violent crime — 19 of 134 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Hampden safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
