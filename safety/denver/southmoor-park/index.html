Is Southmoor Park safe?

Southmoor Park sits at the safer end of Denver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 85 out of 100 — 30 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 4th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 63 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Southmoor Park safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Southmoor Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Denver, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Southmoor Park?

    Other theft — 21 of 63 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Southmoor Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
