    Common questions

    Is University Park safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in University Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in University Park?

    Other theft — 91 of 250 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the University Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
