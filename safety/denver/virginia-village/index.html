Is Virginia Village safe?

Virginia Village sits at the safer end of Denver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 9th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 107 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Virginia Village safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Virginia Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Denver, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Virginia Village?

    Vehicle crime — 31 of 107 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Virginia Village safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
