Is Westwood safe?

Westwood records more reported crime than most Denver neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 8 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 52nd of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 392 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Westwood safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 29% of severity-weighted incidents in Westwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Westwood?

    Violent crime — 52 of 392 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Westwood safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
