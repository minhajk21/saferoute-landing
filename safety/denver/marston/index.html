Is Marston safe?

Marston sits at the safer end of Denver by reported crime.     Common questions

    Is Marston safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 90/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Marston are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Denver, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Marston?

    Vehicle crime — 17 of 44 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Marston safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
