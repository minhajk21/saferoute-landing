Is Capitol Hill safe?

Capitol Hill records a high level of reported crime for Denver. Its SafeRoute safety index is 11 out of 100 — 44 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 73rd of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 3,022 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Capitol Hill safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 11/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Capitol Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Capitol Hill?

    Drugs — 668 of 3,022 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Capitol Hill safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
