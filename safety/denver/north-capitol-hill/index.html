    Common questions

    Is North Capitol Hill safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 10/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in North Capitol Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in North Capitol Hill?

    Violent crime — 382 of 3,351 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the North Capitol Hill safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
