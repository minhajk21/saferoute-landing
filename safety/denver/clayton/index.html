Is Clayton safe?

Clayton records more reported crime than most Denver neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 45 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 54th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 430 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Clayton safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Clayton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Clayton?

    Violent crime — 45 of 430 incidents (10%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Clayton safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
