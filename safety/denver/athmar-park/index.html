    Common questions

    Is Athmar Park safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Athmar Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Athmar Park?

    Public order — 48 of 293 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Athmar Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
