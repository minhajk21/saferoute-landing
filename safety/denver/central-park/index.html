Is Central Park safe?

Central Park sits at the safer end of Denver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 67 out of 100 — 12 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 18th of 76 Denver neighborhoods — based on 182 incidents reported to the Denver Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Central Park safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Central Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Central Park?

    Vehicle crime — 67 of 182 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Central Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Denver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Denver, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
