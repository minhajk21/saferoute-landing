Common questions

Is Champion Hill safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Champion Hill?

Violent crime — 102 of 338 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the Champion Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
