Is Hampstead Town safe?

Hampstead Town sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 13 points above the Inner London median of 31, ranking 10th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 126 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through April 2026).

Common questions

Is Hampstead Town safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hampstead Town?

Violent crime — 29 of 126 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through April 2026.

How is the Hampstead Town safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
