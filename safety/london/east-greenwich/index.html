Is East Greenwich safe?

East Greenwich sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 73 out of 100 — 14 points above the Inner London median of 59, ranking 50th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 262 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is East Greenwich safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Greenwich?

Violent crime — 72 of 262 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the East Greenwich safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
