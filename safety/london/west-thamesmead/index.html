Is West Thamesmead safe?

West Thamesmead sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 24 points above the Inner London median of 54, ranking 20th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 145 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through May 2026).

How is the West Thamesmead safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
