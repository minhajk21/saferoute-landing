Is Ravenscourt safe?

Ravenscourt sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Common questions

Is Ravenscourt safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ravenscourt?

Violent crime — 69 of 321 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Ravenscourt safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
