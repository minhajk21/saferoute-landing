Is Canary Wharf safe?

Canary Wharf records more reported crime than most London neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 49 out of 100 — 5 points below the Inner London median of 54, ranking 153rd of 248 Inner London areas — based on 573 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through May 2026).

Common questions

Is Canary Wharf safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Canary Wharf?

Violent crime — 152 of 573 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the Canary Wharf safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
