Is King's Park safe?

King's Park sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 18 points above the Inner London median of 54, ranking 37th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 212 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through May 2026).

Common questions

Is King's Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in King's Park?

Violent crime — 54 of 212 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the King's Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
