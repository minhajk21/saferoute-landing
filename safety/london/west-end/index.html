Is West End safe?

West End records a high level of reported crime for Inner London. Its SafeRoute safety index is 14 out of 100 — 17 points below the Inner London median of 31, ranking 248th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 2,562 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through April 2026).

Common questions

Is West End safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 14/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West End?

Theft from a person — 660 of 2,562 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through April 2026.

How is the West End safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
