Is East Putney safe?

East Putney sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 36 out of 100 — 5 points above the Inner London median of 31, ranking 55th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 244 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through April 2026).

Is East Putney safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Putney?

Violent crime — 68 of 244 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through April 2026.

How is the East Putney safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
