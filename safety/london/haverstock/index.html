Common questions

Is Haverstock safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Haverstock?

Violent crime — 128 of 571 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Haverstock safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
