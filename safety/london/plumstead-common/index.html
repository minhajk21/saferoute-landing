Is Plumstead Common safe?

Plumstead Common sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 79 out of 100 — 20 points above the Inner London median of 59, ranking 28th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 169 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

How is the Plumstead Common safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
