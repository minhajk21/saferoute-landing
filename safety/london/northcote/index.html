Is Northcote safe?

Northcote sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 18 points above the Inner London median of 59, ranking 35th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 217 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). Reported crime is low for Inner London, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Northcote?

Violent crime — 43 of 217 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Northcote safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
