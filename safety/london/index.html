    Methodology

    Each incident published by the police (via data.police.uk, Open Government Licence) is weighted by severity — violence counts for more than shoplifting. For every ward we sum weighted incidents within 1 km of its centre (ONS December 2025 ward centroids), and normalise onto a 0–100 index, higher = safer. Incident locations are anonymised by the police to nearby snap points, so the maps show streets rather than exact addresses. Pages regenerate as new data is published.

    Sources. Crime data: street-level crime via data.police.uk (Open Government Licence v3.0), data through April 2026. Ward boundaries: ONS, December 2025. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Analysis © SafeRoute.

    About this data. Figures are incidents reported to police within 1 km of each neighbourhood's centre — reporting practices vary and not all crime is reported. This is informational only and not a guarantee of safety, a prediction, or a judgment of any community. Use it the way the app does: to pick better-lit, lower-incident routes and times.
