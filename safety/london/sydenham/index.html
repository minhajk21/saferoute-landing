Is Sydenham safe?

Sydenham sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 75 out of 100 — 16 points above the Inner London median of 59, ranking 46th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 218 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

What is the most common crime in Sydenham?

Violent crime — 70 of 218 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Sydenham safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
