Is Battersea Park safe?

Battersea Park sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 15 points above the Inner London median of 59, ranking 47th of 248 Inner London areas — based on 242 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Battersea Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Battersea Park?

Violent crime — 64 of 242 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Battersea Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
