Is Greenwich Park safe?

Greenwich Park sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 15 points above the Inner London median of 54, ranking 43rd of 248 Inner London areas — based on 261 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through May 2026).

Common questions

Is Greenwich Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greenwich Park?

Violent crime — 63 of 261 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the Greenwich Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
