Is West Dulwich safe?

West Dulwich sits at the safer end of Inner London by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 20 points above the Inner London median of 49, ranking 33rd of 248 Inner London areas — based on 211 incidents reported to the police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through May 2026).

Common questions

Is West Dulwich safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Dulwich?

Violent crime — 45 of 211 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the West Dulwich safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalises against national crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
