Is Beacon Hill safe?

Beacon Hill records a high level of reported crime for Boston. Its SafeRoute safety index is 33 out of 100 — 22 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 23rd of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 1,490 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Beacon Hill safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Beacon Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Beacon Hill?

Violent crime — 303 of 1,490 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Beacon Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
