Is Hyde Park safe?

Hyde Park shows a typical level of reported crime for Boston. Common questions

Is Hyde Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in Hyde Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hyde Park?

Other theft — 107 of 400 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Hyde Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
