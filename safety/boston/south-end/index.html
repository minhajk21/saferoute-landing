Is South End safe?

South End records a high level of reported crime for Boston. Its SafeRoute safety index is 31 out of 100 — 24 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 25th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 1,543 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South End safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in South End are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South End?

Violent crime — 279 of 1,543 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South End safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
