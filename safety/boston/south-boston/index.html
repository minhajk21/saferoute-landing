Is South Boston safe?

South Boston sits at the safer end of Boston by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 67 out of 100 — 12 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 6th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 322 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Boston safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in South Boston are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Boston?

Violent crime — 70 of 322 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Boston safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
