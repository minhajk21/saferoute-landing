Is Allston safe?

Allston sits at the safer end of Boston by reported crime. Common questions

Is Allston safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Allston are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Boston, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Allston?

Violent crime — 41 of 242 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Allston safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
