Is Back Bay safe?

Back Bay records more reported crime than most Boston neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 43 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 18th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 1,322 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Back Bay safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Back Bay are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Back Bay?

Violent crime — 155 of 1,322 incidents (12%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Back Bay safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
