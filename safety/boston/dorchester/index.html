Is Dorchester safe?

Dorchester records more reported crime than most Boston neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 35 out of 100 — 20 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 21st of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 1,400 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Dorchester safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Dorchester are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Dorchester?

Violent crime — 160 of 1,400 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Dorchester safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
