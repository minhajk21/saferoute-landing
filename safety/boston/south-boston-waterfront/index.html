Is South Boston Waterfront safe?

South Boston Waterfront sits at the safer end of Boston by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 87 out of 100 — 32 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 2nd of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 92 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Boston Waterfront safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 87/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in South Boston Waterfront are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Boston, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in South Boston Waterfront?

Violent crime — 27 of 92 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Boston Waterfront safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
