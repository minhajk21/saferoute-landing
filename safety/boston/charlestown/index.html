Is Charlestown safe?

Charlestown sits at the safer end of Boston by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 4th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 237 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Charlestown safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Charlestown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Boston, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Charlestown?

Violent crime — 32 of 237 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Charlestown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
