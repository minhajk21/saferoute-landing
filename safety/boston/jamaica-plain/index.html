Common questions

Is Jamaica Plain safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Jamaica Plain are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Jamaica Plain?

Other theft — 231 of 701 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Jamaica Plain safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Boston, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
