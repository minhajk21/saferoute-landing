Is Fenway safe?

Fenway shows a typical level of reported crime for Boston. Its SafeRoute safety index is 61 out of 100 — 6 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 11th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 514 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Fenway safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Fenway are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Fenway?

Violent crime — 79 of 514 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Fenway safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
