Is Leather District safe?

Leather District records more reported crime than most Boston neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 36 out of 100 — 19 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 20th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 1,260 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Leather District safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Leather District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Leather District?

Violent crime — 292 of 1,260 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Leather District safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
