Is West End safe?

West End records more reported crime than most Boston neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 48 out of 100 — 7 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 16th of 25 Boston neighborhoods — based on 761 incidents reported to the Boston Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is West End safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in West End are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West End?

Violent crime — 152 of 761 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West End safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Boston Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
