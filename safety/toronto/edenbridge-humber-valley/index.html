Is Edenbridge-Humber Valley safe?

Edenbridge-Humber Valley sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 86 out of 100 — 32 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 5th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 33 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Edenbridge-Humber Valley safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 86/100). About 80% of severity-weighted incidents in Edenbridge-Humber Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Edenbridge-Humber Valley?

Burglary — 14 of 33 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Edenbridge-Humber Valley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
