Is Trinity-Bellwoods safe?

Trinity-Bellwoods records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 27 out of 100 — 29 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 143rd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 483 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Trinity-Bellwoods safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Trinity-Bellwoods are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Trinity-Bellwoods?

Violent crime — 320 of 483 incidents (66%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Trinity-Bellwoods safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
