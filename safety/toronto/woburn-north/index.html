Common questions

Is Woburn North safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 50/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Woburn North are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Woburn North?

Violent crime — 107 of 201 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Woburn North safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
