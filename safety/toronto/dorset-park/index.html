Common questions

Is Dorset Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Dorset Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Dorset Park?

Violent crime — 56 of 140 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Dorset Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
