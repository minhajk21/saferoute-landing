Is Wychwood safe?

Wychwood records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 119th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 248 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Wychwood safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Wychwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Wychwood?

Violent crime — 141 of 248 incidents (57%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Wychwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
