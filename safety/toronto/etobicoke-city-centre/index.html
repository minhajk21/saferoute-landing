Is Etobicoke City Centre safe?

Etobicoke City Centre sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 8th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 50 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Etobicoke City Centre safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Etobicoke City Centre are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Etobicoke City Centre?

Violent crime — 17 of 50 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Etobicoke City Centre safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
