Is Hillcrest Village safe?

Hillcrest Village sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 15th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 54 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Hillcrest Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Hillcrest Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Hillcrest Village?

Violent crime — 18 of 54 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Hillcrest Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
