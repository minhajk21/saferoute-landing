Is North St.James Town safe?

North St.James Town records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Common questions

Is North St.James Town safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 12/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in North St.James Town are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North St.James Town?

Violent crime — 826 of 1,174 incidents (70%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the North St.James Town safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
