Common questions

Is Oakwood Village safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 53/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Oakwood Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Oakwood Village?

Violent crime — 99 of 175 incidents (57%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Oakwood Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
