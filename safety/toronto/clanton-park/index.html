Common questions

Is Clanton Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Clanton Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Clanton Park?

Violent crime — 62 of 110 incidents (56%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Clanton Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
