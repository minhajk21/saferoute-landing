Is Stonegate-Queensway safe?

Stonegate-Queensway sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 68 out of 100 — 14 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 39th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 92 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Stonegate-Queensway safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Stonegate-Queensway are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Stonegate-Queensway?

Violent crime — 39 of 92 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Stonegate-Queensway safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
