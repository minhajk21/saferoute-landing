Is Downtown Yonge East safe?

Downtown Yonge East records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 8 out of 100 — 46 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 157th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 1,918 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Downtown Yonge East safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 8/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Downtown Yonge East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Downtown Yonge East?

Violent crime — 1,377 of 1,918 incidents (72%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Downtown Yonge East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
