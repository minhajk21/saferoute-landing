Is Oakdale-Beverley Heights safe?

Oakdale-Beverley Heights records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 38 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 127th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 297 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Is Oakdale-Beverley Heights safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 38/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Oakdale-Beverley Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Oakdale-Beverley Heights?

Violent crime — 157 of 297 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Oakdale-Beverley Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
