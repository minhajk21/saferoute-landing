Common questions

Is St.Andrew-Windfields safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in St.Andrew-Windfields are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in St.Andrew-Windfields?

Violent crime — 19 of 46 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the St.Andrew-Windfields safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
