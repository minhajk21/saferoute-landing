Is Bedford Park-Nortown safe?

Bedford Park-Nortown shows a typical level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 67 out of 100 — 13 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 42nd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 109 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Bedford Park-Nortown safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Bedford Park-Nortown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bedford Park-Nortown?

Burglary — 35 of 109 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Bedford Park-Nortown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
