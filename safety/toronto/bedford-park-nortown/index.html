Is Bedford Park-Nortown safe?

Bedford Park-Nortown sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 68 out of 100 — 14 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 36th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 99 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 9 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Bedford Park-Nortown safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 63% of severity-weighted incidents in Bedford Park-Nortown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bedford Park-Nortown?

Violent crime — 22 of 99 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 9 months to June 2026.

How is the Bedford Park-Nortown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
