Is Mount Pleasant East safe?

Mount Pleasant East records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 118th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 253 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Mount Pleasant East safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Mount Pleasant East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mount Pleasant East?

Violent crime — 122 of 253 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Mount Pleasant East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
