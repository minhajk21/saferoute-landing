Is South Riverdale safe?

South Riverdale sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 92 out of 100 — 36 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 4th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 19 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is South Riverdale safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 92/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in South Riverdale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in South Riverdale?

Violent crime — 8 of 19 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the South Riverdale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
