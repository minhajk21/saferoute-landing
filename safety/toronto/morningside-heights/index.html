Common questions

Is Morningside Heights safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 97/100). About 100% of severity-weighted incidents in Morningside Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Morningside Heights?

Violent crime — 3 of 6 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through May 2026.

How is the Morningside Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
