Is North Riverdale safe?

North Riverdale records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 40 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 124th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 259 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is North Riverdale safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in North Riverdale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Riverdale?

Violent crime — 159 of 259 incidents (61%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the North Riverdale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
