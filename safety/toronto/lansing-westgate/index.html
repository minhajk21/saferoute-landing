Is Lansing-Westgate safe?

Lansing-Westgate sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 12th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 54 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Lansing-Westgate safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Lansing-Westgate are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Lansing-Westgate?

Violent crime — 24 of 54 incidents (44%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Lansing-Westgate safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
