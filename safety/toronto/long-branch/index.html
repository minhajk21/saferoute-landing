Common questions

Is Long Branch safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Long Branch are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Long Branch?

Violent crime — 52 of 96 incidents (54%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Long Branch safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
