Is Guildwood safe?

Guildwood sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 79 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 11th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 46 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Guildwood safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 79/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Guildwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Guildwood?

Violent crime — 25 of 46 incidents (54%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Guildwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
