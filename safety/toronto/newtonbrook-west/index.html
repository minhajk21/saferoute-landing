Is Newtonbrook West safe?

Newtonbrook West sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 73 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 24th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 71 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Newtonbrook West safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Newtonbrook West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Newtonbrook West?

Violent crime — 34 of 71 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Newtonbrook West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
