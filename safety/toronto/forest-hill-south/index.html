Is Forest Hill South safe?

Forest Hill South sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 32nd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 85 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Forest Hill South safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Forest Hill South are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Forest Hill South?

Violent crime — 36 of 85 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Forest Hill South safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
