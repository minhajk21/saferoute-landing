Is Wellington Place safe?

Wellington Place records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 13 out of 100 — 41 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 152nd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 1,155 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Wellington Place safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 13/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Wellington Place are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Wellington Place?

Violent crime — 769 of 1,155 incidents (67%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Wellington Place safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
