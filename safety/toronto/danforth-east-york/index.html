Is Danforth East York safe?

Danforth East York records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 13 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 120th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 243 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Danforth East York safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Danforth East York are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Danforth East York?

Violent crime — 163 of 243 incidents (67%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Danforth East York safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
