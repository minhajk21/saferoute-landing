Is Humber Bay Shores safe?

Humber Bay Shores records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Common questions

Is Humber Bay Shores safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Humber Bay Shores are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Humber Bay Shores?

Violent crime — 118 of 215 incidents (55%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Humber Bay Shores safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
