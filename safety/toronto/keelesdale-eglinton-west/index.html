Is Keelesdale-Eglinton West safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Keelesdale-Eglinton West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Keelesdale-Eglinton West?

Violent crime — 95 of 166 incidents (57%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Keelesdale-Eglinton West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
