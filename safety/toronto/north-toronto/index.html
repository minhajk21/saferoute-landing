Is North Toronto safe?

North Toronto records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 32 out of 100 — 22 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 136th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 330 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 9 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is North Toronto safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in North Toronto are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Toronto?

Violent crime — 197 of 330 incidents (60%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 9 months to June 2026.

How is the North Toronto safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
