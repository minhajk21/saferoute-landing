Common questions

Is Mount Dennis safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Mount Dennis are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mount Dennis?

Violent crime — 77 of 125 incidents (62%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Mount Dennis safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
