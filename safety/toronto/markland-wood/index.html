Is Markland Wood safe?

Markland Wood sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 75 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 23rd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 76 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Markland Wood safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Markland Wood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Markland Wood?

Violent crime — 24 of 76 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Markland Wood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
