Common questions

Is L'Amoreaux West safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in L'Amoreaux West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in L'Amoreaux West?

Violent crime — 81 of 179 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the L'Amoreaux West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
