Common questions

Is Malvern East safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 56/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Malvern East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Malvern East?

Violent crime — 92 of 147 incidents (63%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Malvern East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
