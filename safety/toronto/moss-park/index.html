Is Moss Park safe?

Moss Park records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 11 out of 100 — 43 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 155th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 1,335 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Moss Park safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 11/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Moss Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Moss Park?

Violent crime — 937 of 1,335 incidents (70%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Moss Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
