Common questions

Is Bendale South safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Bendale South are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bendale South?

Violent crime — 91 of 175 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Bendale South safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
