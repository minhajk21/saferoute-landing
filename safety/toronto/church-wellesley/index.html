Common questions

Is Church-Wellesley safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 9/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Church-Wellesley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Church-Wellesley?

Violent crime — 1,147 of 1,623 incidents (71%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Church-Wellesley safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
