Is Alderwood safe?

Alderwood sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 16th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 61 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Alderwood safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Alderwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Alderwood?

Violent crime — 28 of 61 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Alderwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
