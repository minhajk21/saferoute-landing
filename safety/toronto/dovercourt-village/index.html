Is Dovercourt Village safe?

Dovercourt Village records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 35 out of 100 — 19 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 133rd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 309 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Dovercourt Village safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Dovercourt Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Dovercourt Village?

Violent crime — 215 of 309 incidents (70%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Dovercourt Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
