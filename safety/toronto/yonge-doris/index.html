Is Yonge-Doris safe?

Yonge-Doris records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 39 out of 100 — 17 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 129th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 291 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Yonge-Doris safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Yonge-Doris are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Yonge-Doris?

Violent crime — 184 of 291 incidents (63%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Yonge-Doris safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
