Is Lawrence Park South safe?

Lawrence Park South shows a typical level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 62 out of 100 — 8 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 58th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 119 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Lawrence Park South safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 62/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Lawrence Park South are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lawrence Park South?

Violent crime — 51 of 119 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Lawrence Park South safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
