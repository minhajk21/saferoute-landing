Is Kensington-Chinatown safe?

Kensington-Chinatown records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Its SafeRoute safety index is 13 out of 100 — 43 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 151st of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 1,169 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Kensington-Chinatown safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 13/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Kensington-Chinatown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Kensington-Chinatown?

Violent crime — 759 of 1,169 incidents (65%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Kensington-Chinatown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
