Is Roncesvalles safe?

Roncesvalles records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Common questions

Is Roncesvalles safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Roncesvalles are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Roncesvalles?

Violent crime — 219 of 338 incidents (65%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Roncesvalles safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
