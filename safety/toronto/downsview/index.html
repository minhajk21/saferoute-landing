Is Downsview safe?

Downsview sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Common questions

Is Downsview safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Downsview are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Downsview?

Violent crime — 34 of 62 incidents (55%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Downsview safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
