Is Oakridge safe?

Oakridge records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 37 out of 100 — 19 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 131st of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 317 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Oakridge safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 37/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in Oakridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Oakridge?

Violent crime — 199 of 317 incidents (63%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Oakridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
