Is Yonge-Eglinton safe?

Yonge-Eglinton records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 36 out of 100 — 18 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 132nd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 308 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Yonge-Eglinton safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Yonge-Eglinton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Yonge-Eglinton?

Violent crime — 181 of 308 incidents (59%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Yonge-Eglinton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
