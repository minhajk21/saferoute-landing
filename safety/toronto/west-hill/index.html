Is West Hill safe?

West Hill records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 9 points below the citywide median of 56, ranking 102nd of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 203 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is West Hill safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in West Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Hill?

Violent crime — 143 of 203 incidents (70%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the West Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
