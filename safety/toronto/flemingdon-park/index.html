Is Flemingdon Park safe?

Flemingdon Park records more reported crime than most Toronto neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 110th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 220 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Flemingdon Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Flemingdon Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Flemingdon Park?

Violent crime — 145 of 220 incidents (66%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Flemingdon Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
