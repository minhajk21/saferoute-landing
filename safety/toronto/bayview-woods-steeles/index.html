Common questions

Is Bayview Woods-Steeles safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Bayview Woods-Steeles are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Toronto, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Bayview Woods-Steeles?

Violent crime — 20 of 47 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 10 months to June 2026.

How is the Bayview Woods-Steeles safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Toronto, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
