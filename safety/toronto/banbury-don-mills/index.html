Is Banbury-Don Mills safe?

Banbury-Don Mills sits at the safer end of Toronto by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 13 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 37th of 158 Toronto neighbourhoods — based on 107 incidents reported to the Toronto Police Service within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Banbury-Don Mills safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Banbury-Don Mills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Banbury-Don Mills?

Violent crime — 31 of 107 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the Banbury-Don Mills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
