Is University safe?

University records a high level of reported crime for Toronto. Common questions

Is University safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 21/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in University are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in University?

Violent crime — 424 of 677 incidents (63%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre through June 2026.

How is the University safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Toronto Police Service by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
