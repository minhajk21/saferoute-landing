    Common questions

    Is Mission Beach safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 28/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in Mission Beach are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Mission Beach?

    Violent crime — 69 of 284 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the community center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Mission Beach safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the San Diego Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the community center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Diego, so scores compare communities to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
