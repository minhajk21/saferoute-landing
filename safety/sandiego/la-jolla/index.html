Is La Jolla safe?

La Jolla sits at the safer end of San Diego by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 79 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 10th of 53 San Diego communities — based on 33 incidents reported to the San Diego Police within 1 km of the community center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is La Jolla safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 79/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in La Jolla are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for San Diego, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in La Jolla?

    Other theft — 10 of 33 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the community center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the La Jolla safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the San Diego Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the community center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within San Diego, so scores compare communities to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
