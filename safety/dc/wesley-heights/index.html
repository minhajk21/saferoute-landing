Is Wesley Heights safe?

Wesley Heights sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 86 out of 100 — 33 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 11th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 43 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Wesley Heights safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 86/100). About 20% of severity-weighted incidents in Wesley Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Wesley Heights?

Vehicle crime — 23 of 43 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Wesley Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
