Is Van Ness safe?

Van Ness sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Common questions

Is Van Ness safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Van Ness are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Van Ness?

Other theft — 55 of 105 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Van Ness safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
