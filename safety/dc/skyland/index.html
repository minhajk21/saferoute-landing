Is Skyland safe?

Skyland records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 11 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 97th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 302 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Skyland safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Skyland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Skyland?

Other theft — 148 of 302 incidents (49%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Skyland safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
