Common questions

Is Capitol View safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 60/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Capitol View are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Capitol View?

Violent crime — 28 of 172 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Capitol View safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
