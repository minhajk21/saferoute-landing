Common questions

Is Marshall Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Marshall Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Marshall Heights?

Violent crime — 35 of 165 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Marshall Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
