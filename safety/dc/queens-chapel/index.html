Common questions

Is Queens Chapel safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Queens Chapel are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Queens Chapel?

Vehicle crime — 88 of 178 incidents (49%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Queens Chapel safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
