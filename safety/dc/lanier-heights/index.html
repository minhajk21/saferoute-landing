Is Lanier Heights safe?

Lanier Heights records a high level of reported crime for Washington, D.C.. Common questions

Is Lanier Heights safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 22/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in Lanier Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lanier Heights?

Other theft — 627 of 848 incidents (74%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lanier Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
