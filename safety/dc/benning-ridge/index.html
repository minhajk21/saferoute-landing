Common questions

Is Benning Ridge safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Benning Ridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Benning Ridge?

Violent crime — 25 of 138 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Benning Ridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
