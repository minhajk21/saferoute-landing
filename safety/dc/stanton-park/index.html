Is Stanton Park safe?

Stanton Park records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Stanton Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Stanton Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Stanton Park?

Other theft — 251 of 504 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Stanton Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
