Is Garfield Heights safe?

Garfield Heights records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 105th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 408 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

Common questions

Is Garfield Heights safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Garfield Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Garfield Heights?

Other theft — 190 of 408 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Garfield Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
