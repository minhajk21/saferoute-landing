Is North Michigan Park safe?

North Michigan Park sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 68 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 28th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 110 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is North Michigan Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in North Michigan Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Michigan Park?

Vehicle crime — 69 of 110 incidents (63%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Michigan Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
