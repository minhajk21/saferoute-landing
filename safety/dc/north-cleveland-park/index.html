Common questions

Is North Cleveland Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in North Cleveland Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Cleveland Park?

Other theft — 151 of 218 incidents (69%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Cleveland Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
