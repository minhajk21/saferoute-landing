Is Cathedral Heights safe?

Cathedral Heights sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 70 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 24th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 112 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Cathedral Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in Cathedral Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Cathedral Heights?

Other theft — 74 of 112 incidents (66%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Cathedral Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
