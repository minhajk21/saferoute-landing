Common questions

Is Navy Yard safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 58/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Navy Yard are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Navy Yard?

Other theft — 144 of 249 incidents (58%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Navy Yard safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
