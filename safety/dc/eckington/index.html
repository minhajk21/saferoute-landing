Is Eckington safe?

Eckington records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 105th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 379 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Eckington safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Eckington are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Eckington?

Other theft — 176 of 379 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Eckington safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
