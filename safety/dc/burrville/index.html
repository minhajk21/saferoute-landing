Common questions

Is Burrville safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 63/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Burrville are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Burrville?

Other theft — 57 of 153 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Burrville safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
