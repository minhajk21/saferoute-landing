Is Langdon safe?

Langdon shows a typical level of reported crime for Washington, D.C.. Common questions

Is Langdon safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 63/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Langdon are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Langdon?

Vehicle crime — 67 of 161 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Langdon safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
