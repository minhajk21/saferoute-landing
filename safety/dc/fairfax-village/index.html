Is Fairfax Village safe?

Fairfax Village sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 82 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 14th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 67 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Fairfax Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 82/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Fairfax Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Fairfax Village?

Vehicle crime — 32 of 67 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Fairfax Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
