Common questions

Is Buzzard Point safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 62% of severity-weighted incidents in Buzzard Point are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Buzzard Point?

Other theft — 61 of 129 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Buzzard Point safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
