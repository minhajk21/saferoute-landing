Is Brightwood Park safe?

Brightwood Park records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 48 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 92nd of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 343 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Brightwood Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Brightwood Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brightwood Park?

Other theft — 171 of 343 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Brightwood Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
