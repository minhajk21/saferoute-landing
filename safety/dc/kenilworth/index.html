Is Kenilworth safe?

Kenilworth sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 70 out of 100 — 12 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 33rd of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 104 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Kenilworth safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Kenilworth are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Kenilworth?

Other theft — 35 of 104 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Kenilworth safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
