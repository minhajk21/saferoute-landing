Is Near Southeast safe?

Near Southeast records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 49 out of 100 — 9 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 90th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 330 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Near Southeast safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Near Southeast are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Near Southeast?

Other theft — 214 of 330 incidents (65%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Near Southeast safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
