Common questions

Is Truxton Circle safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Truxton Circle are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Truxton Circle?

Other theft — 374 of 699 incidents (54%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Truxton Circle safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
