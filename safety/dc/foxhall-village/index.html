Is Foxhall Village safe?

Foxhall Village sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Common questions

Is Foxhall Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 86/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Foxhall Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Foxhall Village?

Vehicle crime — 23 of 41 incidents (56%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Foxhall Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
