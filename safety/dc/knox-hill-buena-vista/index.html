Is Knox Hill/Buena Vista safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Knox Hill/Buena Vista are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Knox Hill/Buena Vista?

Other theft — 181 of 357 incidents (51%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the Knox Hill/Buena Vista safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
