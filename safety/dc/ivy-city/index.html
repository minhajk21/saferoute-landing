Is Ivy City safe?

Ivy City records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 50 out of 100 — 8 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 87th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 284 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Ivy City safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 50/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Ivy City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ivy City?

Other theft — 121 of 284 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Ivy City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
