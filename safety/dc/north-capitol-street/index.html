Common questions

Is North Capitol Street safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in North Capitol Street are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Capitol Street?

Other theft — 429 of 729 incidents (59%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the North Capitol Street safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
