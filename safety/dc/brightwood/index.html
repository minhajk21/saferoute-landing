Is Brightwood safe?

Brightwood records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 98th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 388 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Brightwood safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Brightwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brightwood?

Other theft — 227 of 388 incidents (59%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Brightwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
