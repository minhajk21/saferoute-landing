Is Howard University safe?

Howard University records a high level of reported crime for Washington, D.C.. Common questions

Is Howard University safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 28/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Howard University are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Howard University?

Other theft — 243 of 569 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Howard University safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
