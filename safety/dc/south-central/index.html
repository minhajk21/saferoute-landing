Is South Central safe?

South Central shows a typical level of reported crime for Washington, D.C.. Its SafeRoute safety index is 65 out of 100 — 12 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 38th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 122 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Central safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in South Central are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Central?

Vehicle crime — 73 of 122 incidents (60%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Central safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
