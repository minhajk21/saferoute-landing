Common questions

Is Le Droit Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Le Droit Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Le Droit Park?

Other theft — 296 of 644 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Le Droit Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
