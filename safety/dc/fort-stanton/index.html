Is Fort Stanton safe?

Fort Stanton records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Fort Stanton safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Fort Stanton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Fort Stanton?

Violent crime — 46 of 223 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Fort Stanton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
