Is Glover Park safe?

Glover Park sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 14 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 30th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 133 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Glover Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Glover Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Glover Park?

Other theft — 97 of 133 incidents (73%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Glover Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
