Common questions

Is Randle Highlands safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Randle Highlands are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Randle Highlands?

Other theft — 83 of 204 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Randle Highlands safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
