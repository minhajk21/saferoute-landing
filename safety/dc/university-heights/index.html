Common questions

Is University Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in University Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in University Heights?

Vehicle crime — 86 of 198 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

How is the University Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
