Common questions

Is Monumental Core safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 96/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Monumental Core are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Monumental Core?

Other theft — 8 of 15 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Monumental Core safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
