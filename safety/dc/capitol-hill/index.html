Is Capitol Hill safe?

Capitol Hill records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 99th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 393 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Capitol Hill safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Capitol Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Capitol Hill?

Other theft — 242 of 393 incidents (62%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Capitol Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
