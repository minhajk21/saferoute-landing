Is Douglass safe?

Douglass records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Douglass safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Douglass are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Douglass?

Violent crime — 39 of 244 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Douglass safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
