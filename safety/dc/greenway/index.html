Common questions

Is Greenway safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Greenway are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greenway?

Violent crime — 20 of 95 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Greenway safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
