Is Forest Hills safe?

Forest Hills sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 18th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 83 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Forest Hills safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 30% of severity-weighted incidents in Forest Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Forest Hills?

Other theft — 42 of 83 incidents (51%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Forest Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
