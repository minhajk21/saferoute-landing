Common questions

Is 16th Street Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in 16th Street Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in 16th Street Heights?

Other theft — 96 of 202 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the 16th Street Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
