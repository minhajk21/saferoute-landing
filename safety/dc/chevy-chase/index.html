Is Chevy Chase safe?

Chevy Chase sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 71 out of 100 — 13 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 31st of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 140 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Chevy Chase safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 71/100). About 24% of severity-weighted incidents in Chevy Chase are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Chevy Chase?

Other theft — 76 of 140 incidents (54%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Chevy Chase safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
