Is Fort McNair safe?

Fort McNair sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 18 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 21st of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 88 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Fort McNair safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Fort McNair are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Fort McNair?

Other theft — 41 of 88 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Fort McNair safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
