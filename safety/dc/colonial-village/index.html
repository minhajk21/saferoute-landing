Is Colonial Village safe?

Common questions

Is Colonial Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 90/100). About 25% of severity-weighted incidents in Colonial Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Colonial Village?

Vehicle crime — 34 of 40 incidents (85%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Colonial Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
