Common questions

Is Hill East safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in Hill East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hill East?

Other theft — 102 of 191 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Hill East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
