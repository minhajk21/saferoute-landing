Is Woodland safe?

Woodland records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 111th of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 353 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Woodland safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Woodland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Woodland?

Other theft — 165 of 353 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Woodland safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
