Is Brentwood safe?

Brentwood records more reported crime than most Washington, D.C. neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 11 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 93rd of 132 D.C. neighborhoods — based on 302 incidents reported to the DC Metropolitan Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Brentwood safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Brentwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brentwood?

Other theft — 169 of 302 incidents (56%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Brentwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
