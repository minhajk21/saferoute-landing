Is Hawthorne safe?

Hawthorne sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Common questions

Is Hawthorne safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 95/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in Hawthorne are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Hawthorne?

Vehicle crime — 17 of 18 incidents (94%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Hawthorne safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
