Is Fort Davis Park safe?

Fort Davis Park sits at the safer end of Washington, D.C. by reported crime. Common questions

Is Fort Davis Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 86/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Fort Davis Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Washington, D.C., but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Fort Davis Park?

Vehicle crime — 34 of 53 incidents (64%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Fort Davis Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the DC Metropolitan Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Washington, D.C., so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
