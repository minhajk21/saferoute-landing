Is Harbor Island safe?

Harbor Island sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 94 out of 100 — 40 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 1st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 20 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Harbor Island safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 94/100). About 70% of severity-weighted incidents in Harbor Island are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Harbor Island?

Other theft — 8 of 20 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Harbor Island safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
