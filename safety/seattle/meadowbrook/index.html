Is Meadowbrook safe?

Meadowbrook sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 83 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 5th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 78 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Meadowbrook safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 83/100). About 62% of severity-weighted incidents in Meadowbrook are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Meadowbrook?

Violent crime — 10 of 78 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Meadowbrook safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
