Is SODO safe?

SODO records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 11 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 61st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 453 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is SODO safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in SODO are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in SODO?

Vehicle crime — 163 of 453 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the SODO safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
