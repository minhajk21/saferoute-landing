Is Fairmount Park safe?

Fairmount Park records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 61st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 476 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Fairmount Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Fairmount Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Fairmount Park?

Vehicle crime — 119 of 476 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Fairmount Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
