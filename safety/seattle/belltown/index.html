Is Belltown safe?

Belltown records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 11 out of 100 — 43 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 88th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 3,086 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Belltown safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 11/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Belltown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Belltown?

Vehicle crime — 908 of 3,086 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Belltown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
