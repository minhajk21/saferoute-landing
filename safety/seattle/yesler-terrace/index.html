Common questions

Is Yesler Terrace safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 10/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Yesler Terrace are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Yesler Terrace?

Violent crime — 406 of 3,072 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Yesler Terrace safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
