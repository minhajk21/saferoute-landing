Is West Queen Anne safe?

West Queen Anne shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is West Queen Anne safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in West Queen Anne are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Queen Anne?

Burglary — 87 of 435 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the West Queen Anne safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
