Is Lawton Park safe?

Lawton Park sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 11th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 104 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Lawton Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Lawton Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Lawton Park?

Vehicle crime — 48 of 104 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lawton Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
