Is Riverview safe?

Riverview sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 79 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 15th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 97 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Riverview safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 79/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Riverview are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Riverview?

Violent crime — 13 of 97 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Riverview safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
