Is University of Washington safe?

University of Washington records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 45 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 64th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 548 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is University of Washington safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in University of Washington are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in University of Washington?

Other theft — 119 of 548 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the University of Washington safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
