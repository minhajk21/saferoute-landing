Is West Woodland safe?

West Woodland records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 32 out of 100 — 25 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 79th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 859 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is West Woodland safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in West Woodland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Woodland?

Burglary — 144 of 859 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Woodland safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
