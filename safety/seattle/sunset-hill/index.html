Is Sunset Hill safe?

Sunset Hill sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 21st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 119 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Sunset Hill safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 76% of severity-weighted incidents in Sunset Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Sunset Hill?

Vehicle crime — 46 of 119 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Sunset Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
