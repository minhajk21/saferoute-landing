Is Minor safe?

Minor records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is Minor safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 20/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Minor are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Minor?

Violent crime — 153 of 1,312 incidents (12%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Minor safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
