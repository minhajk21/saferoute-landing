Common questions

Is Denny Triangle safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 9/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Denny Triangle are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Denny Triangle?

Violent crime — 392 of 3,702 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Denny Triangle safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
