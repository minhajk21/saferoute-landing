Is South Lake Union safe?

South Lake Union records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 20 out of 100 — 37 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 85th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 1,521 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Lake Union safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 20/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in South Lake Union are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Lake Union?

Burglary — 286 of 1,521 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Lake Union safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
