Is Bitter Lake safe?

Bitter Lake shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is Bitter Lake safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Bitter Lake are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bitter Lake?

Other theft — 88 of 408 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Bitter Lake safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
