Is First Hill safe?

First Hill records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 9 out of 100 — 45 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 91st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 3,440 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is First Hill safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 9/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in First Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in First Hill?

Violent crime — 419 of 3,440 incidents (12%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the First Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
