Is Rainier View safe?

Rainier View sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 82 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 7th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 82 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Rainier View safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 82/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Rainier View are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Rainier View?

Vehicle crime — 34 of 82 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Rainier View safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
