Is Phinney Ridge safe?

Phinney Ridge shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 61 out of 100 — 7 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 37th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 234 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Phinney Ridge safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 70% of severity-weighted incidents in Phinney Ridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Phinney Ridge?

Vehicle crime — 115 of 234 incidents (49%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Phinney Ridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
