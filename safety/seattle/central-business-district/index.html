Is Central Business District safe?

Central Business District records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 8 out of 100 — 46 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 93rd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 4,233 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Central Business District safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 8/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Central Business District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Central Business District?

Violent crime — 546 of 4,233 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Central Business District safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
