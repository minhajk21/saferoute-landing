Is South Beacon Hill safe?

South Beacon Hill sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 23 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 16th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 99 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is South Beacon Hill safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in South Beacon Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in South Beacon Hill?

Violent crime — 14 of 99 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the South Beacon Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
