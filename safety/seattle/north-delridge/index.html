Common questions

Is North Delridge safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 58/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in North Delridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Delridge?

Vehicle crime — 76 of 267 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Delridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
