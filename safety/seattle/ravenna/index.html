Is Ravenna safe?

Ravenna shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 59 out of 100 — 5 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 42nd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 267 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Ravenna safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 67% of severity-weighted incidents in Ravenna are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ravenna?

Vehicle crime — 133 of 267 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Ravenna safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
