Is Broadview safe?

Broadview sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 81 out of 100 — 27 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 4th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 88 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Broadview safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 81/100). About 70% of severity-weighted incidents in Broadview are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Broadview?

Vehicle crime — 30 of 88 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Broadview safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
