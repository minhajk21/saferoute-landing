Common questions

Is Brighton safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Brighton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brighton?

Violent crime — 56 of 411 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Brighton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
