Is Whittier Heights safe?

Whittier Heights shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is Whittier Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in Whittier Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Whittier Heights?

Vehicle crime — 123 of 401 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Whittier Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
