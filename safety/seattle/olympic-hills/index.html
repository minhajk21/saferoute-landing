Is Olympic Hills safe?

Olympic Hills records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 58th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 391 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Olympic Hills safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Olympic Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Olympic Hills?

Violent crime — 61 of 391 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Olympic Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
