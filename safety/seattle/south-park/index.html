Is South Park safe?

South Park shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 24th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 130 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in South Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Park?

Violent crime — 14 of 130 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
