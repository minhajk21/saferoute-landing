Is Licton Springs safe?

Licton Springs records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Licton Springs safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Licton Springs are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Licton Springs?

Violent crime — 69 of 517 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Licton Springs safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
