Is Briarcliff safe?

Briarcliff sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Common questions

Is Briarcliff safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 71% of severity-weighted incidents in Briarcliff are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Briarcliff?

Burglary — 35 of 120 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Briarcliff safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
