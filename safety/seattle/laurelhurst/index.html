Is Laurelhurst safe?

Laurelhurst sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 13th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 108 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Laurelhurst safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Laurelhurst are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Laurelhurst?

Vehicle crime — 35 of 108 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Laurelhurst safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
