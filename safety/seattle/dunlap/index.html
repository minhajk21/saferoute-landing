Is Dunlap safe?

Dunlap records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 13 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 63rd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 469 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Dunlap safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Dunlap are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Dunlap?

Violent crime — 84 of 469 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Dunlap safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
