Is Montlake safe?

Montlake sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 73 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 21st of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 134 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Montlake safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Montlake are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Montlake?

Vehicle crime — 58 of 134 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Montlake safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
