Common questions

Is Pike-Market safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 10/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Pike-Market are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pike-Market?

Violent crime — 316 of 2,917 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Pike-Market safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
