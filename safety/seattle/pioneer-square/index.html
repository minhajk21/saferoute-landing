Is Pioneer Square safe?

Pioneer Square records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 13 out of 100 — 41 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 87th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 2,081 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Pioneer Square safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 13/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Pioneer Square are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pioneer Square?

Violent crime — 346 of 2,081 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Pioneer Square safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
