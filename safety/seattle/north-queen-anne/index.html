Is North Queen Anne safe?

North Queen Anne shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 48 out of 100 — 6 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 53rd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 388 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is North Queen Anne safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 67% of severity-weighted incidents in North Queen Anne are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Queen Anne?

Vehicle crime — 150 of 388 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the North Queen Anne safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
