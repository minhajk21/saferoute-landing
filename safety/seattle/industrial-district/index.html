Is Industrial District safe?

Industrial District sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 18 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 23rd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 138 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Industrial District safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Industrial District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Industrial District?

Burglary — 21 of 138 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Industrial District safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
