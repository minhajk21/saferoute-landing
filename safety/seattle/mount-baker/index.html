Is Mount Baker safe?

Mount Baker records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 15 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 69th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 485 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Mount Baker safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Mount Baker are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mount Baker?

Violent crime — 54 of 485 incidents (11%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Mount Baker safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
