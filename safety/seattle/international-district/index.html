Is International District safe?

International District records a high level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is International District safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 12/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in International District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in International District?

Violent crime — 369 of 2,621 incidents (14%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the International District safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
