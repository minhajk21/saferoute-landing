Is Mid-Beacon Hill safe?

Mid-Beacon Hill sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 75 out of 100 — 21 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 17th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 125 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Mid-Beacon Hill safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 68% of severity-weighted incidents in Mid-Beacon Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Mid-Beacon Hill?

Vehicle crime — 44 of 125 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Mid-Beacon Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
