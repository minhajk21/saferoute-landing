Is Columbia City safe?

Columbia City records more reported crime than most Seattle neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 59th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 431 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Columbia City safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Columbia City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Columbia City?

Criminal damage & arson — 57 of 431 incidents (13%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Columbia City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
