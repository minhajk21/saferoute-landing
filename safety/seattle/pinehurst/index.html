Is Pinehurst safe?

Pinehurst shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 26th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 145 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Pinehurst safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 63% of severity-weighted incidents in Pinehurst are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pinehurst?

Burglary — 24 of 145 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Pinehurst safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
