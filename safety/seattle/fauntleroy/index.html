Is Fauntleroy safe?

Fauntleroy sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 7th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 105 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Fauntleroy safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 66% of severity-weighted incidents in Fauntleroy are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Fauntleroy?

Vehicle crime — 45 of 105 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

How is the Fauntleroy safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
