Is North Beach/Blue Ridge safe?

North Beach/Blue Ridge sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 83 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 6th of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 81 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is North Beach/Blue Ridge safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 83/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in North Beach/Blue Ridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Seattle, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in North Beach/Blue Ridge?

Vehicle crime — 32 of 81 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Beach/Blue Ridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
