Is North Admiral safe?

North Admiral shows a typical level of reported crime for Seattle. Common questions

Is North Admiral safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). About 63% of severity-weighted incidents in North Admiral are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Admiral?

Vehicle crime — 60 of 190 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Admiral safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
