Is Gatewood safe?

Gatewood sits at the safer end of Seattle by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 18 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 22nd of 94 Seattle neighborhoods — based on 149 incidents reported to the SPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is Gatewood safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 67% of severity-weighted incidents in Gatewood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Gatewood?

Vehicle crime — 59 of 149 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Gatewood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Seattle Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Seattle, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
