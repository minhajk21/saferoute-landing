Airport Area sits at the safer end of Long Beach by reported crime.     Common questions

    Is Airport Area safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 20% of severity-weighted incidents in Airport Area are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Long Beach, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Airport Area?

    Vehicle crime — 14 of 33 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Airport Area safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
