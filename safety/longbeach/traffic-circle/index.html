Is Traffic Circle safe?

Traffic Circle records more reported crime than most Long Beach neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 13 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 65th of 96 Long Beach neighborhoods — based on 272 incidents reported to the Long Beach Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Traffic Circle safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Traffic Circle are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Traffic Circle?

    Violent crime — 76 of 272 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Traffic Circle safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
