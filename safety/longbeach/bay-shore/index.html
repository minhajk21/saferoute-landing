    Common questions

    Is Bay Shore safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 30% of severity-weighted incidents in Bay Shore are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Bay Shore?

    Violent crime — 27 of 107 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Bay Shore safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
