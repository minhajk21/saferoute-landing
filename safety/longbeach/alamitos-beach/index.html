    Is Alamitos Beach safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 20/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in Alamitos Beach are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Alamitos Beach?

    Violent crime — 269 of 824 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Alamitos Beach safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
