Is Carroll Park safe?

Carroll Park records more reported crime than most Long Beach neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 27 out of 100 — 30 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 81st of 96 Long Beach neighborhoods — based on 530 incidents reported to the Long Beach Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Carroll Park safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Carroll Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Carroll Park?

    Violent crime — 190 of 530 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Carroll Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
