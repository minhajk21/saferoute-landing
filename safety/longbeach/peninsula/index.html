Is Peninsula safe?

Peninsula sits at the safer end of Long Beach by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 20 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 21st of 96 Long Beach neighborhoods — based on 62 incidents reported to the Long Beach Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Peninsula safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Peninsula are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Long Beach, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Peninsula?

    Violent crime — 17 of 62 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Peninsula safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
