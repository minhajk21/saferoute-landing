    Common questions

    Is Paramount safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 27% of severity-weighted incidents in Paramount are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Paramount?

    Violent crime — 73 of 228 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Paramount safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
