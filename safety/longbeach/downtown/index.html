Is Downtown safe?

Downtown records a high level of reported crime for Long Beach. Its SafeRoute safety index is 13 out of 100 — 44 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 95th of 96 Long Beach neighborhoods — based on 1,457 incidents reported to the Long Beach Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Downtown safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 13/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Downtown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Downtown?

    Violent crime — 473 of 1,457 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to August 2026.

    How is the Downtown safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Long Beach Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Long Beach, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
