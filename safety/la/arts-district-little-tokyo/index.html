Is Arts District Little Tokyo safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 19/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Arts District Little Tokyo are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Arts District Little Tokyo?

Violent crime — 445 of 1,441 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Arts District Little Tokyo safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
