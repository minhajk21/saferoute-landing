Common questions

Is Panorama City safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Panorama City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Panorama City?

Violent crime — 676 of 2,191 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Panorama City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
