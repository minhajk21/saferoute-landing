Common questions

Is Mar Vista safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Mar Vista are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mar Vista?

Other theft — 109 of 219 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Mar Vista safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
