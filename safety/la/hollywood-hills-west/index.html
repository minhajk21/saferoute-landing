Common questions

Is Hollywood Hills West safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 21/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Hollywood Hills West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hollywood Hills West?

Violent crime — 322 of 1,468 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Hollywood Hills West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
