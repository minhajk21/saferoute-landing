Is Venice safe?

Venice shows a typical level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Its SafeRoute safety index is 59 out of 100 — 4 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 46th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 297 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Venice safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Venice are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Venice?

Violent crime — 53 of 297 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Venice safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
