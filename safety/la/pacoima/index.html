Is Pacoima safe?

Pacoima sits at the safer end of Los Angeles by reported crime. Common questions

Is Pacoima safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Pacoima are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Pacoima?

Violent crime — 42 of 153 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Pacoima safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
