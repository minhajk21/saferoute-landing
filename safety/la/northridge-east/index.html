Common questions

Is Northridge East safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Northridge East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Northridge East?

Violent crime — 179 of 865 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Northridge East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
