Is Hollywood United safe?

Hollywood United records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Hollywood United safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Hollywood United are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hollywood United?

Violent crime — 130 of 611 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Hollywood United safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
