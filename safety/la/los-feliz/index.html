Is Los Feliz safe?

Los Feliz records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 36 out of 100 — 22 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 80th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 749 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Los Feliz safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Los Feliz are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Los Feliz?

Violent crime — 127 of 749 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Los Feliz safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
