Common questions

Is Central San Pedro safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Central San Pedro are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Central San Pedro?

Violent crime — 113 of 368 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Central San Pedro safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
