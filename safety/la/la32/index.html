Is LA32 safe?

Common questions

Is LA32 safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in LA32 are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in LA32?

Violent crime — 37 of 170 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the LA32 safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
