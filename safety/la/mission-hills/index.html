Common questions

Is Mission Hills safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Mission Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mission Hills?

Violent crime — 34 of 226 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Mission Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
