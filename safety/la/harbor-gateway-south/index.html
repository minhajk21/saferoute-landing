Is Harbor Gateway South safe?

Common questions

Is Harbor Gateway South safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 85/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Harbor Gateway South are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Harbor Gateway South?

Violent crime — 17 of 97 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Harbor Gateway South safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
