Common questions

Is Lincoln Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Lincoln Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lincoln Heights?

Violent crime — 99 of 398 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lincoln Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
