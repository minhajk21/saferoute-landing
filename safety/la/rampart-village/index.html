Common questions

Is Rampart Village safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Rampart Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Rampart Village?

Violent crime — 277 of 986 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Rampart Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
