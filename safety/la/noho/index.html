Is NoHo safe?

NoHo records a high level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Its SafeRoute safety index is 30 out of 100 — 25 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 86th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 865 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is NoHo safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in NoHo are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in NoHo?

Violent crime — 200 of 865 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the NoHo safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
