Common questions

Is North Westwood safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in North Westwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in North Westwood?

Violent crime — 38 of 209 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the North Westwood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
