Common questions

Is Westlake North safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 14/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Westlake North are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Westlake North?

Violent crime — 562 of 2,278 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Westlake North safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
