Is Mid-City safe?

Mid-City records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Mid-City safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Mid-City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mid-City?

Violent crime — 639 of 2,134 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Mid-City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
