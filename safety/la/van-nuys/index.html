Is Van Nuys safe?

Van Nuys records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 39 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 76th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 600 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Van Nuys safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Van Nuys are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Van Nuys?

Violent crime — 156 of 600 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Van Nuys safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
