Common questions

Is Chatsworth safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Chatsworth are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Chatsworth?

Violent crime — 50 of 250 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Chatsworth safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
