Is Central Hollywood safe?

Central Hollywood records a high level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Its SafeRoute safety index is 19 out of 100 — 36 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 95th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 1,629 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Central Hollywood safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 19/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Central Hollywood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Central Hollywood?

Violent crime — 375 of 1,629 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Central Hollywood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
