Common questions

Is Wilshire Center Koreatown safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 20/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Wilshire Center Koreatown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Wilshire Center Koreatown?

Violent crime — 1,888 of 6,329 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Wilshire Center Koreatown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
