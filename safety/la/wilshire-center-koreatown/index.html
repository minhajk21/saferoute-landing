Common questions

Is Wilshire Center Koreatown safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 21/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Wilshire Center Koreatown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Wilshire Center Koreatown?

Violent crime — 319 of 1,393 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Wilshire Center Koreatown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
