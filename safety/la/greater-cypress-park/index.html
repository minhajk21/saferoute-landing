Is Greater Cypress Park safe?

Greater Cypress Park sits at the safer end of Los Angeles by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 75 out of 100 — 20 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 9th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 124 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Greater Cypress Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Cypress Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Greater Cypress Park?

Violent crime — 27 of 124 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Greater Cypress Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
