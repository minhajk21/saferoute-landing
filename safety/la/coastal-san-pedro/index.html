Common questions

Is Coastal San Pedro safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Coastal San Pedro are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Coastal San Pedro?

Violent crime — 71 of 215 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Coastal San Pedro safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
