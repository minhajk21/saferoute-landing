Common questions

Is North Hills East safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in North Hills East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Hills East?

Violent crime — 139 of 553 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Hills East safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
