Is Watts safe?

Watts records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 51, ranking 71st of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 2,064 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Watts safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Watts are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Watts?

Violent crime — 714 of 2,064 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Watts safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
