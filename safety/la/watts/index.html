Is Watts safe?

Watts records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 73rd of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 488 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Watts safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Watts are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Watts?

Violent crime — 166 of 488 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Watts safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
