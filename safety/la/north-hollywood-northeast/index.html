Common questions

Is North Hollywood Northeast safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 64/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in North Hollywood Northeast are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Hollywood Northeast?

Violent crime — 91 of 343 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the North Hollywood Northeast safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
