Is Mid City West safe?

Mid City West records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 49 out of 100 — 6 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 63rd of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 536 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Mid City West safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Mid City West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Mid City West?

Other theft — 133 of 536 incidents (25%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Mid City West safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
