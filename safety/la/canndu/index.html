Common questions

Is CANNDU safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 37/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in CANNDU are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in CANNDU?

Violent crime — 232 of 676 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the CANNDU safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
