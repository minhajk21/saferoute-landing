Is CANNDU safe?

CANNDU records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 33 out of 100 — 18 points below the citywide median of 51, ranking 81st of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 3,037 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is CANNDU safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in CANNDU are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in CANNDU?

Violent crime — 1,128 of 3,037 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the CANNDU safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
