Is Encino safe?

Encino sits at the safer end of Los Angeles by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 16 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 22nd of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 214 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Encino safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Encino are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Encino?

Shoplifting — 110 of 214 incidents (51%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Encino safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
