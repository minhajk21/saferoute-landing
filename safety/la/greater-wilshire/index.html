Common questions

Is Greater Wilshire safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Wilshire are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greater Wilshire?

Violent crime — 153 of 783 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Greater Wilshire safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
