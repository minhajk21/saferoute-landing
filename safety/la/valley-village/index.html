Is Valley Village safe?

Common questions

Is Valley Village safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Valley Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Valley Village?

Other theft — 142 of 338 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Valley Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
