Is Westchester/Playa safe?

Westchester/Playa records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Westchester/Playa safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Westchester/Playa are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Westchester/Playa?

Other theft — 209 of 496 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Westchester/Playa safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
