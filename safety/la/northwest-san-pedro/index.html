Common questions

Is Northwest San Pedro safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Northwest San Pedro are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Northwest San Pedro?

Violent crime — 17 of 147 incidents (12%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Northwest San Pedro safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
