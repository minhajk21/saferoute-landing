Is South Central safe?

South Central records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 32 out of 100 — 23 points below the citywide median of 55, ranking 83rd of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 722 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is South Central safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in South Central are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Central?

Violent crime — 245 of 722 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Central safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
