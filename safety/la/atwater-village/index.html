Is Atwater Village safe?

Atwater Village sits at the safer end of Los Angeles by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 15th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 173 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Atwater Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 39% of severity-weighted incidents in Atwater Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Atwater Village?

Violent crime — 28 of 173 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Atwater Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
