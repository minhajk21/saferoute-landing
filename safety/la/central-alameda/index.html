Is Central Alameda safe?

Central Alameda records more reported crime than most Los Angeles neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 51, ranking 68th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 2,130 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through August 2026).

Common questions

Is Central Alameda safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Central Alameda are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Central Alameda?

Violent crime — 822 of 2,130 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Central Alameda safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
