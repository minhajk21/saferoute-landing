Is Harbor City safe?

Harbor City shows a typical level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Common questions

Is Harbor City safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Harbor City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Harbor City?

Violent crime — 48 of 137 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Harbor City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
