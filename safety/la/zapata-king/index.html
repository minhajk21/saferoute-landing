Common questions

Is Zapata King safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Zapata King are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Zapata King?

Violent crime — 233 of 655 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Zapata King safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
