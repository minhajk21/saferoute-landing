Common questions

Is Palms safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 59/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Palms are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Palms?

Violent crime — 56 of 274 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Palms safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
