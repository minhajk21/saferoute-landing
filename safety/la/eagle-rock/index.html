Is Eagle Rock safe?

Eagle Rock sits at the safer end of Los Angeles by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 18 points above the citywide median of 58, ranking 17th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 177 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Eagle Rock safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Eagle Rock are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Los Angeles, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Eagle Rock?

Other theft — 48 of 177 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Eagle Rock safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
