Is Sunland-Tujunga safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 71/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Sunland-Tujunga are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Sunland-Tujunga?

Violent crime — 167 of 685 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through August 2026.

How is the Sunland-Tujunga safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Los Angeles, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
