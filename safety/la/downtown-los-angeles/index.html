Is Downtown Los Angeles safe?

Downtown Los Angeles records a high level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Its SafeRoute safety index is 14 out of 100 — 44 points below the citywide median of 58, ranking 96th of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 2,344 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Downtown Los Angeles safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 14/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Downtown Los Angeles are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Downtown Los Angeles?

Violent crime — 772 of 2,344 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Downtown Los Angeles safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
