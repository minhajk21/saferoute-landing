Is Greater Toluca Lake safe?

Greater Toluca Lake shows a typical level of reported crime for Los Angeles. Its SafeRoute safety index is 66 out of 100 — 11 points above the citywide median of 55, ranking 31st of 99 LA neighborhoods — based on 206 incidents reported to the LAPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Greater Toluca Lake safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Toluca Lake are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greater Toluca Lake?

Other theft — 71 of 206 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Greater Toluca Lake safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
