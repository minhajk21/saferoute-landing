Common questions

Is Harbor Gateway North safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Harbor Gateway North are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Harbor Gateway North?

Violent crime — 29 of 148 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Harbor Gateway North safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the LAPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
