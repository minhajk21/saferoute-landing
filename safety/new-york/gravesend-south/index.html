Is Gravesend (South) safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Gravesend (South) are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Gravesend (South)?

Violent crime — 74 of 462 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Gravesend (South) safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
