Common questions

What is the safest neighborhood in Manhattan, New York?

Upper East Side-Lenox Hill-Roosevelt Island has the highest SafeRoute safety index in Manhattan, New York, at 48/100 (elevated), from 1,937 reported incidents within 1 km of its center. Upper East Side-Carnegie Hill (47/100) and Inwood (44/100) follow.

Which neighborhood in Manhattan, New York has the most reported crime?

Midtown-Times Square has the lowest index in Manhattan, New York, at 17/100, with 7,504 reported incidents within 1 km of its center. A low score means more is reported around that center, which busy commercial and transport areas produce simply by having more people in them — it is not a measure of how dangerous a resident's street is.

How is the SafeRoute safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The figure shown for Manhattan, New York is the median of its 32 neighborhoods — the borough is not measured separately.

Methodology

Each incident reported to the NYPD (via NYC Open Data) is weighted by severity — violence counts for more than shoplifting. For every neighborhood we sum weighted incidents within 1 km of its center (2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Area centroids), and normalize against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 index, higher = safer. Time-of-day charts use NYPD incident timestamps, severity-weighted. Pages regenerate as new data is published.

The index compares neighborhoods within New York City. It is not comparable between cities: each police force publishes a different set of offences over a different period — New York's figures cannot be read against another city's on the same 0–100 scale.
