Common questions

Is Brighton Beach safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Brighton Beach are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brighton Beach?

Violent crime — 104 of 497 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Brighton Beach safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
