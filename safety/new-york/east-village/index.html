Is East Village safe?

East Village records a high level of reported crime for New York City. Its SafeRoute safety index is 28 out of 100 — 25 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 171st of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 3,904 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is East Village safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 28/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in East Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Village?

Other theft — 1,676 of 3,904 incidents (43%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the East Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
