Is Ozone Park safe?

Ozone Park shows a typical level of reported crime for New York City. Its SafeRoute safety index is 66 out of 100 — 7 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 75th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 476 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Ozone Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Ozone Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ozone Park?

Other theft — 183 of 476 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Ozone Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
