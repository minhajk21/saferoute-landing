Common questions

Is Bensonhurst safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Bensonhurst are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bensonhurst?

Violent crime — 106 of 580 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Bensonhurst safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
