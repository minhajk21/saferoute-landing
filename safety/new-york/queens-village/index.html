Is Queens Village safe?

Queens Village sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 70 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 49th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 640 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Queens Village safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Queens Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Queens Village?

Public order — 186 of 640 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Queens Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
