Is Greenwich Village safe?

Greenwich Village records a high level of reported crime for New York City. Its SafeRoute safety index is 28 out of 100 — 31 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 189th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 2,692 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Greenwich Village safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 28/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Greenwich Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greenwich Village?

Other theft — 1,576 of 2,692 incidents (59%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Greenwich Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
