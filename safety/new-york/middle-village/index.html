Is Middle Village safe?

Middle Village sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 32nd of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 440 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Middle Village safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Middle Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Middle Village?

Other theft — 174 of 440 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Middle Village safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
