Common questions

Is Pelham Gardens safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 58/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Pelham Gardens are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pelham Gardens?

Public order — 232 of 1,024 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Pelham Gardens safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
