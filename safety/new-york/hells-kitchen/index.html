Is Hell's Kitchen safe?

Hell's Kitchen records a high level of reported crime for New York City. Its SafeRoute safety index is 27 out of 100 — 32 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 191st of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 2,591 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Hell's Kitchen safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Hell's Kitchen are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Hell's Kitchen?

Other theft — 1,197 of 2,591 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Hell's Kitchen safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
