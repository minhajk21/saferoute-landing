Common questions

Is Flushing-Willets Point safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Flushing-Willets Point are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Flushing-Willets Point?

Other theft — 868 of 2,302 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Flushing-Willets Point safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
