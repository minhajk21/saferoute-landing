Is Laurelton safe?

Laurelton sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 40 out of 100 — 7 points above the citywide median of 33, ranking 45th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 299 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Laurelton safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Laurelton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Laurelton?

Public order — 69 of 299 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Laurelton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
