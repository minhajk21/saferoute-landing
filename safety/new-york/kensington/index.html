Common questions

Is Kensington safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Kensington are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Kensington?

Other theft — 230 of 827 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Kensington safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
