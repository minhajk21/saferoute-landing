Is Midtown-Times Square safe?

Midtown-Times Square records a high level of reported crime for New York City. Its SafeRoute safety index is 17 out of 100 — 16 points below the citywide median of 33, ranking 197th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 3,711 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Midtown-Times Square safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 17/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Midtown-Times Square are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Midtown-Times Square?

Other theft — 1,960 of 3,711 incidents (53%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Midtown-Times Square safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
