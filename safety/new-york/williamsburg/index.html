Is Williamsburg safe?

Williamsburg shows a typical level of reported crime for New York City. Common questions

Is Williamsburg safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 53/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Williamsburg are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Williamsburg?

Other theft — 361 of 866 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Williamsburg safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
