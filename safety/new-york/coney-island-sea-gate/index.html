Is Coney Island-Sea Gate safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Coney Island-Sea Gate are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Coney Island-Sea Gate?

Violent crime — 137 of 938 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Coney Island-Sea Gate safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
