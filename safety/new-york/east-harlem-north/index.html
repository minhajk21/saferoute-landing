Common questions

Is East Harlem (North) safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in East Harlem (North) are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Harlem (North)?

Violent crime — 344 of 2,123 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the East Harlem (North) safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
