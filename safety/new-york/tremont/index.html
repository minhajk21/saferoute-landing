Common questions

Is Tremont safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 27/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Tremont are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Tremont?

Violent crime — 861 of 3,805 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Tremont safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
