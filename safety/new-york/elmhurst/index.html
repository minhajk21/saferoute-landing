Is Elmhurst safe?

Elmhurst records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 39 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 135th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 2,372 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Elmhurst safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Elmhurst are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Elmhurst?

Other theft — 965 of 2,372 incidents (41%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Elmhurst safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
