Common questions

Is South Richmond Hill safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in South Richmond Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Richmond Hill?

Violent crime — 218 of 1,119 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the South Richmond Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
