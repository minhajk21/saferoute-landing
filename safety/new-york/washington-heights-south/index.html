Common questions

Is Washington Heights (South) safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Washington Heights (South) are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Washington Heights (South)?

Violent crime — 201 of 1,293 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Washington Heights (South) safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
