Is Port Richmond safe?

Port Richmond sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 71 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 56, ranking 48th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 316 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Port Richmond safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 71/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Port Richmond are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Port Richmond?

Violent crime — 73 of 316 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Port Richmond safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
