Is Brownsville safe?

Brownsville records a high level of reported crime for New York City. Common questions

Is Brownsville safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 35/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Brownsville are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brownsville?

Violent crime — 358 of 1,975 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Brownsville safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
