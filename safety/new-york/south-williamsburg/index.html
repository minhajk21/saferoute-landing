Common questions

Is South Williamsburg safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in South Williamsburg are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Williamsburg?

Other theft — 284 of 826 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the South Williamsburg safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
