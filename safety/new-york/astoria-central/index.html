Common questions

Is Astoria (Central) safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Astoria (Central) are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Astoria (Central)?

Other theft — 487 of 1,672 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Astoria (Central) safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
