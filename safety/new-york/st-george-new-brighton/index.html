Is St. George-New Brighton safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in St. George-New Brighton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in St. George-New Brighton?

Violent crime — 204 of 1,203 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the St. George-New Brighton safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
