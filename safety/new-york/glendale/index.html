Is Glendale safe?

Glendale records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 13 points above the citywide median of 33, ranking 23rd of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 159 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Glendale safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Glendale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Glendale?

Other theft — 54 of 159 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Glendale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
