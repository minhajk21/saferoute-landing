Is Auburndale safe?

Auburndale sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 82 out of 100 — 29 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 20th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 344 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Auburndale safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 82/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Auburndale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Auburndale?

Public order — 75 of 344 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Auburndale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
