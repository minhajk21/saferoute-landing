Common questions

Is East Elmhurst safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in East Elmhurst are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Elmhurst?

Other theft — 331 of 1,177 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the East Elmhurst safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
