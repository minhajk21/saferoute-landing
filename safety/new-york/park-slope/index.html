Common questions

Is Park Slope safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Park Slope are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Park Slope?

Other theft — 160 of 448 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Park Slope safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
