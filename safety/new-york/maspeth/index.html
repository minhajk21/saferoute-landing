Is Maspeth safe?

Maspeth records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Maspeth safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Maspeth are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Maspeth?

Public order — 40 of 195 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Maspeth safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
