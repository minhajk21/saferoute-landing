Is Greenpoint safe?

Greenpoint sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 42nd of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 301 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Greenpoint safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 43% of severity-weighted incidents in Greenpoint are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Greenpoint?

Other theft — 108 of 301 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Greenpoint safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
