Common questions

Is Harlem (North) safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Harlem (North) are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Harlem (North)?

Violent crime — 350 of 2,045 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Harlem (North) safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
