Is Jackson Heights safe?

Jackson Heights records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 43 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 119th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 1,946 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Jackson Heights safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Jackson Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Jackson Heights?

Other theft — 729 of 1,946 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Jackson Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
