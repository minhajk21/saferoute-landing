Is Fresh Meadows-Utopia safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Fresh Meadows-Utopia are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Fresh Meadows-Utopia?

Other theft — 151 of 396 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Fresh Meadows-Utopia safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
