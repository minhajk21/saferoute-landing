    Methodology

    Each incident reported to the NYPD (via NYC Open Data) is weighted by severity — violence counts for more than shoplifting. For every neighborhood we sum weighted incidents within 1 km of its center (2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Area centroids), and normalize against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 index, higher = safer. Time-of-day charts use NYPD incident timestamps, severity-weighted. Pages regenerate as new data is published.

    The index compares areas within New York City. It is not comparable between cities: each police force publishes a different set of offences over a different period — New York's figures cannot be read against another city's on the same 0–100 scale.

    Sources. Crime data: NYPD complaint data via NYC Open Data, data through March 2026. Neighborhood boundaries: NYC 2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Areas. Basemap (streets, parks, shoreline): NYC Open Data. Analysis © SafeRoute.

    About this data. Figures are incidents reported to police within 1 km of each neighborhood's center — reporting practices vary and not all crime is reported.
