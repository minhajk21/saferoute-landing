Is Morris Park safe?

Morris Park shows a typical level of reported crime for New York City. Common questions

Is Morris Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Morris Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Morris Park?

Public order — 212 of 946 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Morris Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
