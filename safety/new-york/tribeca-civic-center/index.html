Common questions

Is Tribeca-Civic Center safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 23/100). About 31% of severity-weighted incidents in Tribeca-Civic Center are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Tribeca-Civic Center?

Other theft — 1,163 of 2,002 incidents (58%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Tribeca-Civic Center safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
