Is Tottenville-Charleston safe?

Tottenville-Charleston sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 92 out of 100 — 39 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 6th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 143 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through June 2026).

Common questions

Is Tottenville-Charleston safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 92/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Tottenville-Charleston are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Tottenville-Charleston?

Other theft — 75 of 143 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Tottenville-Charleston safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
