Common questions

Is Cypress Hills safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Cypress Hills are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Cypress Hills?

Violent crime — 334 of 1,408 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through June 2026.

How is the Cypress Hills safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
