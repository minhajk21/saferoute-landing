Is Bellerose safe?

Bellerose sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Common questions

Is Bellerose safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 88/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Bellerose are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Bellerose?

Violent crime — 25 of 125 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Bellerose safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
