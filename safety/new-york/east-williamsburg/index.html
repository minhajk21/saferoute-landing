Is East Williamsburg safe?

East Williamsburg records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Common questions

Is East Williamsburg safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 37/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in East Williamsburg are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in East Williamsburg?

Violent crime — 64 of 425 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the East Williamsburg safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
