Is Jamaica safe?

Jamaica records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 36 out of 100 — 23 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 164th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 1,706 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is Jamaica safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Jamaica are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Jamaica?

Other theft — 531 of 1,706 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Jamaica safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
