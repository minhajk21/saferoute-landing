Common questions

Is Bath Beach safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Bath Beach are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bath Beach?

Other theft — 153 of 478 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Bath Beach safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
