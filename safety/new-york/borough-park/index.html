Is Borough Park safe?

Borough Park sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 73 out of 100 — 20 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 37th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 610 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

Common questions

Is Borough Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 73/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Borough Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Borough Park?

Other theft — 221 of 610 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Borough Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
