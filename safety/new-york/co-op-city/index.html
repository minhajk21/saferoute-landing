Is Co-op City safe?

Co-op City records more reported crime than most New York neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Co-op City safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 37/100). About 36% of severity-weighted incidents in Co-op City are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Co-op City?

Other theft — 212 of 471 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Co-op City safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
