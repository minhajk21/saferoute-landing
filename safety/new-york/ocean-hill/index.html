Common questions

Is Ocean Hill safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Ocean Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ocean Hill?

Violent crime — 660 of 4,113 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Ocean Hill safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
