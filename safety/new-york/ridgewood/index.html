Common questions

Is Ridgewood safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 56/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Ridgewood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ridgewood?

Violent crime — 113 of 733 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the Ridgewood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
