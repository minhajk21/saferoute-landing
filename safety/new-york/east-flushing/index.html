Common questions

Is East Flushing safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in East Flushing are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in East Flushing?

Violent crime — 76 of 365 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the East Flushing safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
