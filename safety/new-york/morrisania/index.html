Common questions

Is Morrisania safe at night?

High risk overall (safety index 23/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Morrisania are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Morrisania?

Violent crime — 961 of 4,757 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 6 months to June 2026.

How is the Morrisania safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within New York, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
