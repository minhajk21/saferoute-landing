Is St. Albans safe?

St. Albans sits at the safer end of New York City by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 40th of 197 NYC neighborhoods — based on 255 incidents reported to the NYPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through March 2026).

Common questions

Is St. Albans safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 78/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in St. Albans are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for New York City, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in St. Albans?

Violent crime — 44 of 255 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through March 2026.

How is the St. Albans safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the NYPD by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
