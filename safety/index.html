Walking in your city at night?

SafeRoute scores every walking route against the same official crime data on this page — and shows how much of each route runs on lit streets. Compare the routes, share your walk, and check in when you arrive. Free, no account.

Sources. Crime data comes from each city's police open-data service and, in the UK, from data.police.uk (Open Government Licence v3.0). Each city page names its source and the period it covers, and the Transparency index measures how well each one publishes.

About this data. Figures are incidents reported to police — reporting practices vary and not all crime is reported. This is informational only and not a guarantee of safety, a prediction, or a judgment of any community.
