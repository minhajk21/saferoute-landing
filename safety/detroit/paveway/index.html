Is Paveway safe?

Paveway records more reported crime than most Detroit neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 54 out of 100 — 5 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 133rd of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 558 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Paveway safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Paveway are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Paveway?

    Violent crime — 195 of 558 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Paveway safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
