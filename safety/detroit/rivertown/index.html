Is Rivertown safe?

Rivertown records more reported crime than most Detroit neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 53 out of 100 — 6 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 142nd of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 659 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Rivertown safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 53/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Rivertown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Rivertown?

    Violent crime — 195 of 659 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Rivertown safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
