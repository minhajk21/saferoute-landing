Is North Campau safe?

North Campau sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 20th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 230 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is North Campau safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in North Campau are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in North Campau?

    Violent crime — 77 of 230 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the North Campau safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
