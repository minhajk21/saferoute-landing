Is Oakwood Heights safe?

Oakwood Heights sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 97 out of 100 — 38 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 1st of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 19 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026. Much of this area was cleared for industry or infrastructure, so the low count reflects how few people are left here as much as how safe the street is.

    Common questions

    Is Oakwood Heights safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 97/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Oakwood Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Oakwood Heights?

    Violent crime — 5 of 19 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Oakwood Heights safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
