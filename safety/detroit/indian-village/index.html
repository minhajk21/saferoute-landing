Is Indian Village safe?

Indian Village sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 67 out of 100 — 8 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 43rd of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 387 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Indian Village safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Indian Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Indian Village?

    Violent crime — 73 of 387 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Indian Village safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
