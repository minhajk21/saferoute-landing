Is Rouge Park safe?

Rouge Park sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 81 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 9th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 164 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Rouge Park safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 81/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Rouge Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Rouge Park?

    Violent crime — 60 of 164 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Rouge Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
