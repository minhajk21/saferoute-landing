Harmony Village records a high level of reported crime for Detroit.     Common questions

    Is Harmony Village safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in Harmony Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Harmony Village?

    Violent crime — 301 of 914 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Harmony Village safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
