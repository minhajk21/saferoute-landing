Is West Outer Drive safe?

West Outer Drive sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 87 out of 100 — 28 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 5th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 101 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    Is West Outer Drive safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 87/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in West Outer Drive are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in West Outer Drive?

    Violent crime — 33 of 101 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the West Outer Drive safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
