Is Virginia Park Community safe?

Virginia Park Community records more reported crime than most Detroit neighborhoods.     Common questions

    Is Virginia Park Community safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Virginia Park Community are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Virginia Park Community?

    Violent crime — 208 of 574 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Virginia Park Community safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
