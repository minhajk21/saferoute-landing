Is Gratiot Town/Kettering safe?

Gratiot Town/Kettering sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 75 out of 100 — 16 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 15th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 237 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Is Gratiot Town/Kettering safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 75/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Gratiot Town/Kettering are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Gratiot Town/Kettering?

    Violent crime — 69 of 237 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Gratiot Town/Kettering safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
