Is McDougall-Hunt safe?

McDougall-Hunt sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 19th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 238 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Is McDougall-Hunt safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in McDougall-Hunt are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in McDougall-Hunt?

    Violent crime — 82 of 238 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the McDougall-Hunt safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
