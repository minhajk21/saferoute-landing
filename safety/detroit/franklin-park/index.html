Is Franklin Park safe?

Franklin Park records a high level of reported crime for Detroit. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 15 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 183rd of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 874 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Franklin Park safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Franklin Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Franklin Park?

    Violent crime — 311 of 874 incidents (36%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Franklin Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
