Is Grand River-St Marys safe?

Grand River-St Marys records a high level of reported crime for Detroit.     Is Grand River-St Marys safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Grand River-St Marys are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Grand River-St Marys?

    Violent crime — 260 of 835 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Grand River-St Marys safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
