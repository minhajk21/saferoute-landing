Is Poletown East safe?

Poletown East sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 91 out of 100 — 32 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 3rd of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 67 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026. Much of this area was cleared for industry or infrastructure, so the low count reflects how few people are left here as much as how safe the street is.

    Common questions

    Is Poletown East safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 91/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Poletown East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Poletown East?

    Violent crime — 20 of 67 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Poletown East safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
