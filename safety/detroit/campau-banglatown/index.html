Is Campau/Banglatown safe?

Campau/Banglatown sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 78 out of 100 — 19 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 14th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 189 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Is Campau/Banglatown safe at night?

    What is the most common crime in Campau/Banglatown?

    How is the Campau/Banglatown safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
