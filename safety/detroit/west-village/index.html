    Common questions

    Is West Village safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in West Village are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in West Village?

    Violent crime — 137 of 588 incidents (23%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the West Village safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
