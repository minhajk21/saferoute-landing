    Common questions

    Is Ravendale safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Ravendale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Ravendale?

    Violent crime — 134 of 423 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Ravendale safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
