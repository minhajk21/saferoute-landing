Is Michigan-Martin safe?

Michigan-Martin sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 66 out of 100 — 7 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 49th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 355 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Michigan-Martin safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 66/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Michigan-Martin are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Michigan-Martin?

    Violent crime — 116 of 355 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Michigan-Martin safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
