Is Gratiot Woods safe?

Gratiot Woods sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime.     Is Gratiot Woods safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Gratiot Woods are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Gratiot Woods?

    Violent crime — 110 of 327 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Gratiot Woods safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
