Is Marina District safe?

Marina District sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 84 out of 100 — 25 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 6th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 145 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Marina District safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 84/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Marina District are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Marina District?

    Violent crime — 43 of 145 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Marina District safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
