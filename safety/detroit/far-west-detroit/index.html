Is Far West Detroit safe?

Far West Detroit sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 83 out of 100 — 24 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 8th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 155 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Far West Detroit safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 83/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Far West Detroit are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Detroit, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Far West Detroit?

    Violent crime — 42 of 155 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Far West Detroit safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
