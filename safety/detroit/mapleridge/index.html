    Common questions

    Is Mapleridge safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Mapleridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Mapleridge?

    Violent crime — 206 of 743 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Mapleridge safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
