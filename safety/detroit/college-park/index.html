Is College Park safe?

College Park records more reported crime than most Detroit neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 54 out of 100 — 5 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 125th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 591 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is College Park safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in College Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in College Park?

    Violent crime — 185 of 591 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the College Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
