Is Tech Town safe?

Tech Town records more reported crime than most Detroit neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 59, ranking 169th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 812 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Tech Town safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Tech Town are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Tech Town?

    Violent crime — 229 of 812 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Tech Town safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
