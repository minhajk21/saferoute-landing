Is Mexicantown safe?

Mexicantown sits at the safer end of Detroit by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 10 points above the citywide median of 59, ranking 34th of 201 Detroit neighborhoods — based on 326 incidents reported to the DPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Mexicantown safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Mexicantown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Mexicantown?

    Violent crime — 107 of 326 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Mexicantown safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Detroit Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Detroit, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
