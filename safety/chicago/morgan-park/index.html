Common questions

Is Morgan Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 38/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Morgan Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Morgan Park?

Violent crime — 132 of 373 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Morgan Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
