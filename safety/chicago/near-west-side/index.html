Common questions

Is Near West Side safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Near West Side are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Near West Side?

Violent crime — 332 of 1,487 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Near West Side safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Chicago, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
