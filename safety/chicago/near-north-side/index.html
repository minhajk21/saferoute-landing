Common questions

Is Near North Side safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Near North Side are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Near North Side?

Violent crime — 671 of 3,237 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Near North Side safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
