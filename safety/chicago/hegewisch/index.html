Common questions

Is Hegewisch safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 81/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Hegewisch are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Hegewisch?

Violent crime — 46 of 166 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Hegewisch safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
