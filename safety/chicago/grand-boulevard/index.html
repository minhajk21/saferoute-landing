Common questions

Is Grand Boulevard safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Grand Boulevard are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Grand Boulevard?

Violent crime — 361 of 1,356 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Grand Boulevard safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
