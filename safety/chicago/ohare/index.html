Is O'Hare safe?

O'Hare sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 39 out of 100 — 6 points above the citywide median of 33, ranking 18th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 357 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is O'Hare safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in O'Hare are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in O'Hare?

Violent crime — 78 of 357 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the O'Hare safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
