Common questions

Is West Garfield Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in West Garfield Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Garfield Park?

Violent crime — 539 of 1,843 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the West Garfield Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Chicago, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
