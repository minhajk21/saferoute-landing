Common questions

Is South Shore safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 26/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in South Shore are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Shore?

Violent crime — 693 of 1,965 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the South Shore safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
