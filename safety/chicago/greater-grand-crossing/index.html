Common questions

Is Greater Grand Crossing safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Grand Crossing are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Greater Grand Crossing?

Violent crime — 490 of 1,444 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Greater Grand Crossing safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
