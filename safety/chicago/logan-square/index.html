Is Logan Square safe?

Logan Square records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 52 out of 100 — 5 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 50th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 982 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Logan Square safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Logan Square are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Logan Square?

Violent crime — 148 of 982 incidents (15%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Logan Square safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
