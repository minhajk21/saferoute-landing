Common questions

Is Bridgeport safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 55/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Bridgeport are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Bridgeport?

Violent crime — 158 of 616 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Bridgeport safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
