Common questions

Is Irving Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 63/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Irving Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Irving Park?

Violent crime — 152 of 570 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to July 2026.

How is the Irving Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Chicago, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
