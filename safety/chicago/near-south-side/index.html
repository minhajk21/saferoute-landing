Is Near South Side safe?

Near South Side records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Near South Side safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Near South Side are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Near South Side?

Violent crime — 238 of 913 incidents (26%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Near South Side safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
