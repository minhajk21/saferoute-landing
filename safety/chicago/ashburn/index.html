Is Ashburn safe?

Ashburn sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 68 out of 100 — 16 points above the citywide median of 52, ranking 18th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 322 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Ashburn safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 68/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Ashburn are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Ashburn?

Violent crime — 103 of 322 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Ashburn safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
