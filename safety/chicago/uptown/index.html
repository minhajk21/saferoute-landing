Is Uptown safe?

Uptown records a high level of reported crime for Chicago. Common questions

Is Uptown safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 25/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Uptown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Uptown?

Violent crime — 412 of 1,528 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Uptown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
