Common questions

Is Armour Square safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Armour Square are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Armour Square?

Violent crime — 128 of 689 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Armour Square safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
