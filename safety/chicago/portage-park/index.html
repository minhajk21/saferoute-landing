Common questions

Is Portage Park safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in Portage Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Portage Park?

Violent crime — 143 of 476 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Portage Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
