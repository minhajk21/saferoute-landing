Is Woodlawn safe?

Woodlawn records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 60th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 1,058 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Woodlawn safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Woodlawn are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Woodlawn?

Violent crime — 334 of 1,058 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Woodlawn safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
