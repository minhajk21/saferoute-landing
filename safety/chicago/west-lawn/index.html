Is West Lawn safe?

West Lawn sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 72 out of 100 — 20 points above the citywide median of 52, ranking 14th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 299 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is West Lawn safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 72/100). About 60% of severity-weighted incidents in West Lawn are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Lawn?

Violent crime — 72 of 299 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Lawn safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
