Is Norwood Park safe?

Common questions

Is Norwood Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 84/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Norwood Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Norwood Park?

Violent crime — 66 of 204 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the Norwood Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Chicago, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
