Is Norwood Park safe?

Norwood Park sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 84 out of 100 — 27 points above the citywide median of 57, ranking 8th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 175 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Norwood Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 84/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Norwood Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Norwood Park?

Violent crime — 56 of 175 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Norwood Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
