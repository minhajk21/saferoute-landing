Is Austin safe?

Austin records a high level of reported crime for Chicago. Its SafeRoute safety index is 40 out of 100 — 17 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 66th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 1,335 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Austin safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Austin are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Austin?

Violent crime — 464 of 1,335 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Austin safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
