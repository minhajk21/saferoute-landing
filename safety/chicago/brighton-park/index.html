Common questions

Is Brighton Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Brighton Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Brighton Park?

Violent crime — 201 of 668 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Brighton Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
