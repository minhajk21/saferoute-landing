Common questions

Is Roseland safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Roseland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Roseland?

Violent crime — 227 of 686 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Roseland safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
