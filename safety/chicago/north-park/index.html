Is North Park safe?

North Park sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 86 out of 100 — 34 points above the citywide median of 52, ranking 5th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 124 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is North Park safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 86/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in North Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in North Park?

Violent crime — 36 of 124 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
