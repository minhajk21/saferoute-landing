Is Clearing safe?

Clearing sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Common questions

Is Clearing safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 59% of severity-weighted incidents in Clearing are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Clearing?

Violent crime — 68 of 256 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Clearing safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
