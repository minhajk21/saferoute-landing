Is Archer Heights safe?

Archer Heights sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 69 out of 100 — 15 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 18th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 415 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Archer Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 69/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Archer Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Archer Heights?

Violent crime — 79 of 415 incidents (19%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Archer Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
