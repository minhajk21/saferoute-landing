Common questions

Is Calumet Heights safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 60/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Calumet Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Calumet Heights?

Violent crime — 135 of 470 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Calumet Heights safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
