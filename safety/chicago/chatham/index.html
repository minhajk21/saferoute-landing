Common questions

Is Chatham safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Chatham are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Chatham?

Violent crime — 245 of 731 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Chatham safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
