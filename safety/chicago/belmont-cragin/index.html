Common questions

Is Belmont Cragin safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 58/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in Belmont Cragin are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Belmont Cragin?

Violent crime — 233 of 763 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Belmont Cragin safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
