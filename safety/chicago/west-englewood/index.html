Common questions

Is West Englewood safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in West Englewood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Englewood?

Violent crime — 340 of 1,022 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Englewood safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
