Is Beverly safe?

Beverly sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Common questions

Is Beverly safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Beverly are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Beverly?

Violent crime — 51 of 188 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Beverly safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
