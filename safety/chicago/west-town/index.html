Is West Town safe?

West Town records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 43 out of 100 — 14 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 63rd of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 1,408 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is West Town safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 43/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in West Town are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Town?

Violent crime — 241 of 1,408 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Town safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
