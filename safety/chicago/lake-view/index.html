Common questions

Is Lake View safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 34/100). About 58% of severity-weighted incidents in Lake View are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Lake View?

Violent crime — 343 of 1,756 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Lake View safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
