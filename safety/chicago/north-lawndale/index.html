Is North Lawndale safe?

North Lawndale records a high level of reported crime for Chicago. Common questions

Is North Lawndale safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 31/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in North Lawndale are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in North Lawndale?

Violent crime — 687 of 2,016 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the North Lawndale safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
