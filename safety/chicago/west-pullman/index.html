Common questions

Is West Pullman safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in West Pullman are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Pullman?

Violent crime — 188 of 635 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Pullman safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
