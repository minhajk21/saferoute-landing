Is Rogers Park safe?

Rogers Park records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 29 out of 100 — 4 points below the citywide median of 33, ranking 53rd of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 964 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center (data through July 2026).

Common questions

Is Rogers Park safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 29/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Rogers Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Rogers Park?

Violent crime — 325 of 964 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Rogers Park safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
