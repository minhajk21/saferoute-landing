Common questions

Is West Ridge safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 54/100). About 57% of severity-weighted incidents in West Ridge are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Ridge?

Violent crime — 201 of 648 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Ridge safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
