Is South Chicago safe?

South Chicago records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 57, ranking 56th of 77 Chicago community areas — based on 1,185 incidents reported to the Chicago Police Department within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

Common questions

Is South Chicago safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in South Chicago are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in South Chicago?

Violent crime — 388 of 1,185 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 7 months to August 2026.

How is the South Chicago safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Chicago, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
