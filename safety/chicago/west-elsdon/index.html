Common questions

Is West Elsdon safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 65/100). About 54% of severity-weighted incidents in West Elsdon are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in West Elsdon?

Violent crime — 150 of 528 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the West Elsdon safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
