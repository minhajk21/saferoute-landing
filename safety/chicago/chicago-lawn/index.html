Common questions

Is Chicago Lawn safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Chicago Lawn are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Chicago Lawn?

Violent crime — 332 of 999 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Chicago Lawn safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
