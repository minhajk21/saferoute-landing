Auburn Gresham records more reported crime than most Chicago neighborhoods. Common questions

Is Auburn Gresham safe at night?

Elevated overall (safety index 36/100). About 48% of severity-weighted incidents in Auburn Gresham are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Auburn Gresham?

Violent crime — 403 of 1,257 incidents (32%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Auburn Gresham safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
