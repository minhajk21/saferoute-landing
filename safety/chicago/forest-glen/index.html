Is Forest Glen safe?

Forest Glen sits at the safer end of Chicago by reported crime. Common questions

Is Forest Glen safe at night?

Low risk overall (safety index 94/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Forest Glen are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Chicago, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

What is the most common crime in Forest Glen?

Violent crime — 11 of 54 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center through July 2026.

How is the Forest Glen safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Chicago Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety.
