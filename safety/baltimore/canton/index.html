Is Canton safe?

Canton records more reported crime than most Baltimore areas. Its SafeRoute safety index is 47 out of 100 — 6 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 34th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 951 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Canton safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Canton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Canton?

    Vehicle crime — 337 of 951 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Canton safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
