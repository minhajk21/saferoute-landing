Is Midway/Coldstream safe?

Midway/Coldstream records more reported crime than most Baltimore areas.     Is Midway/Coldstream safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Midway/Coldstream are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Midway/Coldstream?

    Violent crime — 295 of 876 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Midway/Coldstream safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
