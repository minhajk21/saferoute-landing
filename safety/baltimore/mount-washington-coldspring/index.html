Is Mount Washington/Coldspring safe?

Mount Washington/Coldspring sits at the safer end of Baltimore by reported crime.     Is Mount Washington/Coldspring safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 91/100). About 45% of severity-weighted incidents in Mount Washington/Coldspring are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Baltimore, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Mount Washington/Coldspring?

    Violent crime — 27 of 60 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Mount Washington/Coldspring safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
