Is Claremont/Armistead safe?

Claremont/Armistead sits at the safer end of Baltimore by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 74 out of 100 — 21 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 10th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 200 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Claremont/Armistead safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 74/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Claremont/Armistead are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Claremont/Armistead?

    Violent crime — 87 of 200 incidents (44%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Claremont/Armistead safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
