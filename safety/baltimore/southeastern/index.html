Is Southeastern safe?

Southeastern sits at the safer end of Baltimore by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 91 out of 100 — 38 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 3rd of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 87 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Southeastern safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 91/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in Southeastern are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Baltimore, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Southeastern?

    Violent crime — 14 of 87 incidents (16%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Southeastern safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
