Is Greater Rosemont safe?

Greater Rosemont records more reported crime than most Baltimore areas. Its SafeRoute safety index is 38 out of 100 — 15 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 42nd of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 931 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Greater Rosemont safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 38/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Rosemont are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Greater Rosemont?

    Violent crime — 376 of 931 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Greater Rosemont safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
