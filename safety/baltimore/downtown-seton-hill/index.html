Is Downtown/Seton Hill safe?

Downtown/Seton Hill records a high level of reported crime for Baltimore. Its SafeRoute safety index is 21 out of 100 — 32 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 56th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 2,305 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Downtown/Seton Hill safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 21/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Downtown/Seton Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Downtown/Seton Hill?

    Violent crime — 766 of 2,305 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Downtown/Seton Hill safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
