Is Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill safe?

Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill sits at the safer end of Baltimore by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 94 out of 100 — 41 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 1st of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 46 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 94/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Baltimore, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill?

    Violent crime — 8 of 46 incidents (17%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Greater Roland Park/Poplar Hill safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
