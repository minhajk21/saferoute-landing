    Common questions

    Is Greenmount East safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 32/100). About 52% of severity-weighted incidents in Greenmount East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Greenmount East?

    Violent crime — 494 of 1,269 incidents (39%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Greenmount East safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
