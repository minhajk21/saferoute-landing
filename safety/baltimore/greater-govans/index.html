    Common questions

    Is Greater Govans safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 55% of severity-weighted incidents in Greater Govans are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Greater Govans?

    Violent crime — 225 of 657 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Greater Govans safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
