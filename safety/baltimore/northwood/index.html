Is Northwood safe?

Northwood sits at the safer end of Baltimore by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 70 out of 100 — 17 points above the citywide median of 53, ranking 14th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 276 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Northwood safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 70/100). About 61% of severity-weighted incidents in Northwood are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Northwood?

    Violent crime — 81 of 276 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Northwood safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
