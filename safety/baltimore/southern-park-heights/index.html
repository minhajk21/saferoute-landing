Is Southern Park Heights safe?

Southern Park Heights records more reported crime than most Baltimore areas. Its SafeRoute safety index is 44 out of 100 — 9 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 37th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 753 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Southern Park Heights safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 44/100). About 44% of severity-weighted incidents in Southern Park Heights are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Southern Park Heights?

    Violent crime — 300 of 753 incidents (40%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Southern Park Heights safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
