Is Clifton-Berea safe?

Clifton-Berea records more reported crime than most Baltimore areas. Its SafeRoute safety index is 41 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 53, ranking 40th of 56 Baltimore community statistical areas — based on 868 incidents reported to the Baltimore Police within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    Is Clifton-Berea safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in Clifton-Berea are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Clifton-Berea?

    Violent crime — 326 of 868 incidents (38%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the Clifton-Berea safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
