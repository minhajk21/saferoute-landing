    Common questions

    Is The Waverlies safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 45/100). About 47% of severity-weighted incidents in The Waverlies are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in The Waverlies?

    Violent crime — 215 of 798 incidents (27%) reported within 1 km of the area center over the 7 months to August 2026.

    How is the The Waverlies safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Baltimore Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the area center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Baltimore, so scores compare areas to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
