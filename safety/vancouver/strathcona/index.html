Strathcona records a high level of reported crime for Vancouver.     Common questions

    Is Strathcona safe at night?

    High risk overall (safety index 13/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in Strathcona are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Strathcona?

    Violent crime — 523 of 2,920 incidents (18%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    How is the Strathcona safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Vancouver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Vancouver, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
