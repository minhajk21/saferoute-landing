Is Dunbar-Southlands safe?

Dunbar-Southlands sits at the safer end of Vancouver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 80 out of 100 — 26 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 1st of 22 Vancouver local areas — based on 114 incidents reported to the Vancouver Police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Dunbar-Southlands safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in Dunbar-Southlands are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Vancouver, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Dunbar-Southlands?

    Other theft — 40 of 114 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    How is the Dunbar-Southlands safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Vancouver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Vancouver, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
