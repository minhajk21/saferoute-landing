Is Killarney safe?

Killarney sits at the safer end of Vancouver by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 76 out of 100 — 22 points above the citywide median of 54, ranking 4th of 22 Vancouver local areas — based on 143 incidents reported to the Vancouver Police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Killarney safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 76/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in Killarney are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Vancouver, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Killarney?

    Criminal damage & arson — 34 of 143 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    How is the Killarney safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Vancouver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Vancouver, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
