    Common questions

    Is Sunset safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). About 0% of severity-weighted incidents in Sunset are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Sunset?

    Criminal damage & arson — 89 of 411 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    How is the Sunset safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Vancouver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Vancouver, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
