Is Grandview-Woodland safe?

Grandview-Woodland records more reported crime than most Vancouver neighbourhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 29 out of 100 — 25 points below the citywide median of 54, ranking 18th of 22 Vancouver local areas — based on 1,165 incidents reported to the Vancouver Police within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    Is Grandview-Woodland safe at night?

    What is the most common crime in Grandview-Woodland?

    Other theft — 436 of 1,165 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre over the 11 months to August 2026.

    How is the Grandview-Woodland safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Vancouver Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighbourhood centre, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Vancouver, so scores compare neighbourhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
