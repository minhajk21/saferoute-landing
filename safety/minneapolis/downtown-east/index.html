Is Downtown East safe?

Downtown East records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 40 out of 100 — 21 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 71st of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 663 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Downtown East safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 40/100). About 40% of severity-weighted incidents in Downtown East are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Downtown East?

    Vehicle crime — 321 of 663 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Downtown East safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
