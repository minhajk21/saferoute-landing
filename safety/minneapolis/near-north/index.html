Is Near - North safe?

Near - North records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 48 out of 100 — 13 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 63rd of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 394 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Near - North safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 48/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Near - North are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Near - North?

    Violent crime — 94 of 394 incidents (24%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Near - North safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
