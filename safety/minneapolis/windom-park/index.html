    Common questions

    Is Windom Park safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 61/100). About 32% of severity-weighted incidents in Windom Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Windom Park?

    Vehicle crime — 161 of 332 incidents (48%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Windom Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
