Is University of Minnesota safe?

University of Minnesota records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 57 out of 100 — 4 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 53rd of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 361 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is University of Minnesota safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in University of Minnesota are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in University of Minnesota?

    Vehicle crime — 153 of 361 incidents (42%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the University of Minnesota safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
