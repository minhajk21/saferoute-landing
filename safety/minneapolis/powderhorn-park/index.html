Is Powderhorn Park safe?

Powderhorn Park records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 39 out of 100 — 22 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 73rd of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 620 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Is Powderhorn Park safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 39/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Powderhorn Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Powderhorn Park?

    Vehicle crime — 322 of 620 incidents (52%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Powderhorn Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
