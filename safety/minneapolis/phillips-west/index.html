    Common questions

    Is Phillips West safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Phillips West are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Phillips West?

    Vehicle crime — 372 of 807 incidents (46%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Phillips West safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
