    Common questions

    Is King Field safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 60/100). About 24% of severity-weighted incidents in King Field are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in King Field?

    Vehicle crime — 188 of 296 incidents (64%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the King Field safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
