    Is Midtown Phillips safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 30/100). About 41% of severity-weighted incidents in Midtown Phillips are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Midtown Phillips?

    Vehicle crime — 417 of 917 incidents (45%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Midtown Phillips safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
