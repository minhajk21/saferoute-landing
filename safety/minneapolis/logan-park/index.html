Is Logan Park safe?

Logan Park records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 49 out of 100 — 12 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 62nd of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 463 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Logan Park safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 49/100). About 38% of severity-weighted incidents in Logan Park are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Logan Park?

    Vehicle crime — 274 of 463 incidents (59%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Logan Park safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
