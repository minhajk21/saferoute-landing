Is Holland safe?

Holland records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 51 out of 100 — 10 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 56th of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 405 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Holland safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 51/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Holland are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Holland?

    Vehicle crime — 207 of 405 incidents (51%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Holland safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
