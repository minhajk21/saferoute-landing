Is South Uptown safe?

South Uptown records a high level of reported crime for Minneapolis. Its SafeRoute safety index is 34 out of 100 — 27 points below the citywide median of 61, ranking 77th of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 916 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is South Uptown safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 34/100). About 37% of severity-weighted incidents in South Uptown are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in South Uptown?

    Vehicle crime — 460 of 916 incidents (50%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the South Uptown safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
