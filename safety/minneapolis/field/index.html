Is Field safe?

Field sits at the safer end of Minneapolis by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 77 out of 100 — 16 points above the citywide median of 61, ranking 21st of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 137 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Field safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 77/100). About 24% of severity-weighted incidents in Field are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Minneapolis, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Field?

    Vehicle crime — 89 of 137 incidents (65%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Field safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
