Is East Phillips safe?

East Phillips records a high level of reported crime for Minneapolis.     Is East Phillips safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 37/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in East Phillips are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in East Phillips?

    Vehicle crime — 308 of 660 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the East Phillips safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
