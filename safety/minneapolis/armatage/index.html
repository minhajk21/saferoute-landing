Is Armatage safe?

Armatage sits at the safer end of Minneapolis by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 93 out of 100 — 32 points above the citywide median of 61, ranking 2nd of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 33 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Armatage safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 93/100). About 26% of severity-weighted incidents in Armatage are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Minneapolis, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Armatage?

    Vehicle crime — 19 of 33 incidents (58%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Armatage safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
