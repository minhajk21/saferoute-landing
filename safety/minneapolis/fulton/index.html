Is Fulton safe?

Fulton sits at the safer end of Minneapolis by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 89 out of 100 — 28 points above the citywide median of 61, ranking 5th of 87 Minneapolis neighborhoods — based on 57 incidents reported to the MPD within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Fulton safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 89/100). About 16% of severity-weighted incidents in Fulton are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Minneapolis, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Fulton?

    Vehicle crime — 35 of 57 incidents (61%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Fulton safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
