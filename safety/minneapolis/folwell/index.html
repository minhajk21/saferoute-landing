Is Folwell safe?

Folwell records more reported crime than most Minneapolis neighborhoods.     Common questions

    Is Folwell safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 42% of severity-weighted incidents in Folwell are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Folwell?

    Violent crime — 94 of 427 incidents (22%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Folwell safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
