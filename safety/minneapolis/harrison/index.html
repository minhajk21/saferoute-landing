    Common questions

    Is Harrison safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). About 35% of severity-weighted incidents in Harrison are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Harrison?

    Vehicle crime — 150 of 320 incidents (47%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Harrison safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
