Is Page safe?

Page sits at the safer end of Minneapolis by reported crime.     Common questions

    Is Page safe at night?

    Low risk overall (safety index 80/100). About 23% of severity-weighted incidents in Page are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. Reported crime is low for Minneapolis, but stick to lit, busier streets late.

    What is the most common crime in Page?

    Burglary — 31 of 107 incidents (29%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to August 2026.

    How is the Page safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Minneapolis Police Department by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Minneapolis, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
