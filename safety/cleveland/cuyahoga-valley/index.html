Is Cuyahoga Valley safe?

Cuyahoga Valley sits at the safer end of Cleveland by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 71 out of 100 — 21 points above the citywide median of 50, ranking 2nd of 34 Cleveland neighborhoods — based on 317 incidents reported to the Cleveland Division of Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026. Most of this area is airport or industrial land rather than housing, so the count reflects how few people live here rather than how safe the streets are.

    Common questions

    Is Cuyahoga Valley safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 71/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Cuyahoga Valley are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Cuyahoga Valley?

    Violent crime — 64 of 317 incidents (20%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Cuyahoga Valley safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
