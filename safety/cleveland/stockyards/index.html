    Common questions

    Is Stockyards safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 41/100). About 56% of severity-weighted incidents in Stockyards are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Stockyards?

    Violent crime — 348 of 1,006 incidents (35%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Stockyards safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
