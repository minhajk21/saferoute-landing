Is Old Brooklyn safe?

Old Brooklyn records more reported crime than most Cleveland neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 46 out of 100 — 4 points below the citywide median of 50, ranking 21st of 34 Cleveland neighborhoods — based on 866 incidents reported to the Cleveland Division of Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Old Brooklyn safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 46/100). About 49% of severity-weighted incidents in Old Brooklyn are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Old Brooklyn?

    Violent crime — 258 of 866 incidents (30%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Old Brooklyn safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
