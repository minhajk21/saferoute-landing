    Common questions

    Is University safe at night?

    Moderate overall (safety index 67/100). About 53% of severity-weighted incidents in University are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in University?

    Violent crime — 115 of 342 incidents (34%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the University safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
