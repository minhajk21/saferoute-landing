Is Hough safe?

Hough records more reported crime than most Cleveland neighborhoods. Its SafeRoute safety index is 42 out of 100 — 8 points below the citywide median of 50, ranking 25th of 34 Cleveland neighborhoods — based on 946 incidents reported to the Cleveland Division of Police within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    Common questions

    Is Hough safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 42/100). About 51% of severity-weighted incidents in Hough are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Hough?

    Violent crime — 297 of 946 incidents (31%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Hough safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
