    Common questions

    Is Jefferson safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 47/100). About 46% of severity-weighted incidents in Jefferson are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in Jefferson?

    Violent crime — 234 of 845 incidents (28%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the Jefferson safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
