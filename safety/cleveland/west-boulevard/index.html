    Common questions

    Is West Boulevard safe at night?

    Elevated overall (safety index 33/100). About 50% of severity-weighted incidents in West Boulevard are reported between 6 p.m. and 6 a.m. At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a block or two of detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

    What is the most common crime in West Boulevard?

    Violent crime — 464 of 1,422 incidents (33%) reported within 1 km of the neighborhood center over the 8 months to September 2026.

    How is the West Boulevard safety index calculated?

    SafeRoute weights each incident reported to the Cleveland Division of Police by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last available period within 1 km of the neighborhood center, and normalizes against citywide crime rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cleveland, so scores compare neighborhoods to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
