Is Butetown safe?

Butetown records more reported crime than most Cardiff wards. Its SafeRoute safety index is 52 out of 100 — right at the Cardiff median of 55, ranking 18th of 28 Cardiff wards — based on 150 incidents reported to South Wales Police within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

Common questions

Is Butetown safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 52/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Butetown?

Violent crime — 32 of 150 incidents (21%) reported within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

How is the Butetown safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Cardiff rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cardiff, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
