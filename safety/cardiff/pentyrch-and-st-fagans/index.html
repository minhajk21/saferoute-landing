Is Pentyrch and St Fagans safe?

Pentyrch and St Fagans sits at the safer end of Cardiff by reported crime. Its SafeRoute safety index is 94 out of 100 — 39 points above the Cardiff median of 55, ranking 1st of 28 Cardiff wards — based on 8 incidents reported to South Wales Police within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026. Most of this ward is farmland and village rather than town, so a low count reflects how few people are here rather than how safe the streets are.

How is the Pentyrch and St Fagans safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Cardiff rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cardiff, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
