Common questions

Is Pentwyn safe at night?

Moderate overall (safety index 57/100). At night, prefer lit, busier streets — a short detour often avoids the clusters on the map above.

What is the most common crime in Pentwyn?

Violent crime — 41 of 112 incidents (37%) reported within 1 km of the ward centre over the month to July 2026.

How is the Pentwyn safety index calculated?

SafeRoute weights each police-recorded incident by severity (violence weighs more than shoplifting), sums the last published month within 1 km of the ward centre, and normalises against Cardiff rates onto a 0–100 scale — higher is safer. It describes reported crime only; it is not a guarantee of safety. The scale is calibrated within Cardiff, so scores compare wards to each other and cannot be read against another city's.
