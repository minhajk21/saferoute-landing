    Reading the score

    • 0–24High risk
    • 25–49Elevated
    • 50–74Moderate
    • 75–100Low risk

    Each place is scored for the 1 km around it, by the same engine behind the app's walking routes. A higher index means fewer severity-weighted reported incidents. Scores are calibrated within each city, so compare places in the same city, not across cities.

    Schools

    Switch on Schools to see the state and private schools near an address — 50,516 of them in England, Wales, Northern Ireland, 26 US cities, Toronto, Vancouver and Mexico City — with stage and size where published and, where one exists, the official inspection or rating in its own words. Ratings are never compared across states or countries.

    Starting in central London. Search an address to see the schools around it, and click a pin for its details. Pin colour is the stage, never a rating; hollow pins are private.
