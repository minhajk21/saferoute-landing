    Reading the score

    • 0–24High risk
    • 25–49Elevated
    • 50–74Moderate
    • 75–100Low risk

    Each place is scored for the 1 km around it, by the same engine behind the app's walking routes. A higher index means fewer severity-weighted reported incidents. Scores are calibrated within each city, so compare places in the same city, not across cities.
