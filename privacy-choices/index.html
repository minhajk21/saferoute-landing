Privacy choices

Website analytics

This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, but you can still switch it off.

Count my visits on this website

Checking this browser…

Switching it off keeps one on/off flag in this browser, and nothing else. It applies to this browser on this device only; clearing your site data removes it. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, visits are never counted and you don't need to do anything. The SafeRoute app has its own analytics switch in its settings. The privacy policy explains what is collected and why.

SafeRoute maps crime from official police and open-data sources. Informational only — not a guarantee of safety.
